## Make it human (the notes that matter)

- She talks to **one person**, like a client across the table. Calm, not hyped. A small real smile, not a presenter face.
- Short sentences, one breath each. Cut any take where she sounds like she is reading.
- The hook is the whole game. Shoot 4 to 6 versions of the first two lines and test them against the Brand cut's hook.
- Captions are her actual words, always on. People watch muted.
- Keep the numbers concrete: "thirty minutes", "money back", "you own it". No vague benefit-speak.

## Production

- 9:16, 1080×1920. Keep her face and text out of the top 12% and bottom 18% (safe for caption + CTA).
- Brand blue `#2536F5`, white, ink. Fraunces for big words, JetBrains Mono for numbers.
- Lower-third name tag the first time she is on cam: "Nuramina Afham · CCRO".
- Wardrobe: the blue blazer + white shirt. Plain light-grey or blue backdrop so the dot graphics read.
- Match grade and framing to the Brand cut so the two videos feel like one set.
