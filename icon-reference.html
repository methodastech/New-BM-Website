Premium website process steps

The moving interface scenes on the process cards, each in its own step colour. On a wide screen the steps play in turn, left to right, with the line under each one marking its turn; on a narrow screen they all play together.

  1. 1Discover

    bml-pf--flow-discover

    A discovery call, research into your market and an audit of your site and your competitors.

    You getYour competitor analysis and audit findings

  2. 2Strategy and design

    bml-pf--flow-design

    The strategy first, then a custom design and copy in your voice.

    You getYour conversion plan and the full design to approve

  3. 3Build

    bml-pf--flow-build

    Your CMS, SEO and AIO, forms, WhatsApp and analytics, wired in and tested.

    You getA working site on a private link

  4. 4Launch

    bml-pf--flow-launch

    Checked on real devices and improved until it works, then live on the agreed date.

    You getYour live site and lifetime tech support

    bml-pf--after-fit

  3. 3Fixed scope and quote

    bml-pf--after-quote
