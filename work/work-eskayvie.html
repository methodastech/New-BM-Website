A brand identity manual, stationery and a marketing system for a halal supplement company.

Eskayvie makes clinically formulated, halal-certified supplements and trains the entrepreneurs who sell them. The identity had to carry science and faith in the same breath.

A green leaf grows out of the V: natural ingredients inside a clinical, trustworthy wordmark. Purple carries the credibility; green is kept for life.

Royal purple does the work on paper: a deep field for the card and the folder, white for everything that has to be read.

In market the same system sells Diffenz, the flagship supplement: buntings, lobby screens, posters and posts in one purple voice.
