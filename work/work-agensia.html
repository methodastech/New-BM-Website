Agensia

TechnologyBrand Strategy

A new name, a mark and a brand book for an AI automation company serving regulated enterprises.

Stratiq became Agensia: AI native, no code automation for regulated enterprises, said in two words, intelligent simplicity.

The mark is one curve drawn many times, each pass slightly offset, until it reads as an A.

The five sub-brands were rebuilt off the new mark: Converse, Builder, Knowledge, Immerse and SEAL, eighteen lockups, not one.

Four values carry the whole system. Green is the signal, and it is rationed.
