The Pause

LifestyleBrand IdentityPackaging

A quiet identity, packaging and a brand book for a home fragrance curator.

Modern life is loud. The Pause curates home fragrance and gentle objects for people who want their space to feel lighter, so the identity had to be as quiet as the rooms it lives in.

The P is drawn as a pause: two upright strokes and a held breath between them. The wordmark keeps it at the centre and never raises its voice.

Packaging follows one rule: the box should feel like the product before it is even opened. Matte board, the name embossed tone on tone, nothing in full colour.

Every printed piece keeps the same quiet system: Dust and white, generous margins, the P set in a corner.

Out in the world it asks for attention gently: posts that slow the scroll, a lobby screen that says this space is now yours.
