Altigo

EngineeringNamingBrand Identity

A name, a logo system, a brand book and a 2026 company profile for a Penang lift company.

Buildings are changing faster than the systems that move people inside them. Altigo was named and built for that gap: lifts and escalators engineered for modern infrastructure, from Penang outwards.

The O carries the mark: a red arc and a blue form cut by one diagonal, an upward path between floors. Blue holds the trust; red is kept for the moments that need a response.

Every printed piece runs on Flight Blue, white and the one diagonal, so the brand reads before the name does.

On site the badge does the work: on the back of a uniform, on a staff ID, on the posters by the lift lobby.

For 2026 the company profile was rebuilt around one line: a lift should be the part of a building nobody has to think about.
