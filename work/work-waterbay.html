A logo, a brand book and the launch of STÜHL for a Penang maker of portable dental equipment.

Waterbay builds dental equipment that travels: chairs that fold into clinics, schools and rural outreach programmes. The brand had to look as certified as it is portable.

The colours of Waterbay

A copper-orange W inside an open grey ring: warmth for the patient, the steady circle for the clinician. The ring stays open, so the mark never reads as a closed system.

On paper the orange becomes a gradient, burnt at the base and bright at the top, the way the brand photographs its rooms.

STÜHL, the mobile dental chair, launched under the same system as its own product brand, by Waterbay.
