An identity book, a website and redesign drawings for a modular travel system: one shell, panels that swap.

Luggage sold as a system, not a product. Repair or upgrade the part, never the whole case.

The redesign was locked before tooling: a continuous edge rail, a full-bleed panel face, the case fixed at 56 x 36 x 23 cm.

The site sells it in the order a buyer thinks: the problem with luggage, then the system, then three ways in.
