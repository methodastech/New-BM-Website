A name, an identity and every object in the room for a premium bakery café in Johor Bahru.

A bakery café in Johor Bahru for working professionals and weekend visitors from Singapore: handcrafted bread, specialty coffee and a room people want to stay in.

The colours of Breadly Made

The mark is a loaf: one soft, risen silhouette with a small bite taken out of its edge. Electric indigo carries it; neon lime is kept for the snap.

Every touchpoint was drawn as an object: cups, boxes, bags, trays, and a cast of soft 3D shapes that can turn up anywhere.

Staff wear quiet grey with one indigo mark, so the colour is saved for the food and the room.

The launch spoke in the same voice: something is baking, then open soon.
