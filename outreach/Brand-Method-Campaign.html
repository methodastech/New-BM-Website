The US$1 Billion Giveback.

The businesses we have built have crossed US$1 billion in combined sales. Sixteen years, 100+ brands, and clients that scale into the hundreds of millions a year. To mark it, every tech project goes 50% off, and our best clients get a build for free. 01 · The story we tell

Not cheap. Efficient.

This is the one idea the whole campaign rests on. Everyone on the team says it the same way, because it is the honest reason we can price world-class work this low.

01

Why we can do this

Big agencies are slow and expensive because people do the heavy lifting. Our new system, structure and AI formatting do that lifting instead. Same craft, a fraction of the time. We pass the saving straight to the client.

02

What every build gets

A full audit of where you leak customers. A framework and structure that actually converts. Then high-level design: real animation, interaction, brand scripts that sell, and a premium look that lifts awareness. International quality, local price.

03

Why now

US$1 billion in combined client sales is a real milestone, and a real reason to give back. It earns the discount honestly, rewards the clients who got us here, and opens the door to the next hundred brands.

The line, memorised: “We are not cheap, we are efficient. Our system and AI do the work big agencies bill you months of people-hours for. Same international standard, built in days, and right now, at half the price.”

Why this works · psychology The giveback frames a discount as reciprocity, not desperation: we succeeded, so we share, which raises perceived value instead of lowering it. Naming the real mechanism (system + AI) kills the “cheap = low quality” objection before it forms. The milestone adds proof and a clock: it happened once, the offer ends, act now.

04 · The poster

One image, sent first.

Square 1:1, built for WhatsApp broadcast and Status. This is a live, on-brand mockup: screenshot it, or export at 1080×1080. Edit the text right in this file if a number changes.

How to use them

  1. Pick one per audience. Option 2 (Milestone) to past clients, Option 1 (Number) to a broad prospect blast, Option 3 (Clean) to premium leads and on the site. Or A/B test them.
  2. Send the image first, then the matching WhatsApp script from section 03 underneath.
  3. Screenshot or export at 1080×1080 (1:1). Each is built to read at a glance, thumb-stopping in a chat list.
  4. One variable to swap: if the headline number changes, edit only the big figure and the two offer lines. Everything else is fixed brand furniture.
  5. Locked to brand: blue #2536F5, gold for the milestone, Fraunces for the big type, JetBrains Mono for labels. Do not restyle.

Why three, not one Different buyers price differently. A percentage is the fastest promise the eye can read, so Option 1 converts cold lists. A US$1 billion milestone is social proof that makes the gift feel earned, not desperate, so Option 2 warms past clients. A clean white cut signals premium to buyers who distrust loud discounts, so Option 3 suits high-ticket prospects. Same offer, three psychologies.

Make it human

Short sentences, one breath each. Cut any take where she sounds like she is reading. The hook is the whole game, shoot it 5 or 6 times. Captions always on, people watch muted. Keep the numbers concrete: sixteen years, a hundred brands, thirty minutes, money-back.

Production

9:16, 1080×1920 primary. Face and text out of the top 12% and bottom 18% (safe for caption and CTA). Brand blue #2536F5, white, ink. Fraunces for big words, JetBrains Mono for numbers. Lower-third first time on cam: “Nuramina Afham · CCRO”. Match grade to the showreel so the two feel like one piece.

Why this script converts It opens on a reframe (marketing vs product) that makes the viewer feel understood, then widens the category so we are not compared to a web shop. Proof is shown, not claimed, which beats adjectives. “Earns its keep, not a brochure” is a loss frame: a passive site is money already leaking. The close asks for one small, free, specific next step, the easiest yes.

07 · The campaign flow

Land cheap, expand wide, keep them.

How one 50%-off yes becomes a long client. This is the money logic behind the giveback, read left to right.

Step 1

Reach out

Poster + script on WhatsApp. One clear offer, one keyword reply.

Step 2

Free call

30 minutes. We audit, name the gap, show what we would build.

Step 3

Land

50% off the first project. Fixed price, date in writing, guaranteed.

Step 4

Expand

Same client grows: site → app → portal → game.

Step 5

Keep

Care plan and ads retainer. One-off becomes monthly revenue.

The economics The discount is a customer-acquisition cost, not a loss. A cheap or free first build wins trust fast, and because our system makes delivery cheap to produce, the margin survives. The real return is step 4 and 5: the second, third and fourth project at full price, plus recurring care and ads. We buy the relationship once and earn on it for years.
