Start here

How to use these.

These are starting points, not scripts to send word for word. Read the block, keep the shape, make it sound like you.

Swap brackets

Every [bracket] is a real detail. Never send one with a bracket still in it.

WhatsApp

Warmer and shorter. Break it into 2 or 3 short bubbles, no formal sign-off.

Email

A little more structure, still plain. One clear ask, one clear next step.

Reply fast

Speed is the message. A same-hour reply beats a perfect one that lands tomorrow.

Enquiry to booking

Acknowledge an enquiry.

Someone raised a hand. Reply within the hour, keep it short, and hand them the next step.

New enquiry replyWhatsApp / Email

Thanks for reaching out, [name]. Good timing.

Quickest next step is a short call where we map where your [site / app] is leaking and put a number on it.

Does [day] or [day] work? Takes 20 minutes, and you keep the plan either way.

When: the moment an enquiry lands. Two time options are easier to answer than "when are you free".

Enquiry to booking

Confirm the booking.

Lock the slot in writing so it does not quietly slip.

Booking confirmationWhatsApp / Email

You're booked, [name]: [day, date] at [time].

We'll send a [Meet / Zoom] link and a short reminder the day before.

If there is anything you want us to look at first, send it here.

When: right after they pick a time. A written confirm plus a reminder cuts no-shows.

Enquiry to booking

Send the quote.

Scope, price, timeline, ownership. Four lines, no fog.

Quote / proposalEmail

Here is the scope and the fixed price for [project], [name].

[one-line scope]. [RM amount], live in [timeline]. You own the code, content and data, no lock-in.

Happy to walk through it on a quick call. Shall we send a link?

When: straight after the call, while it is warm. Fixed price and ownership remove the two biggest fears.

Enquiry to booking

Chase the quote.

A quiet quote is not a no. Reopen it with a reason, not a "just checking in".

Quote follow-upWhatsApp / Email

Hi [name], following up on the [project] quote.

Any questions on scope or timing we can clear up? If the budget or timeline needs to flex, tell us and we'll shape it around you.

When: 2 to 3 days after the quote. Offering to flex is what unsticks most silent quotes.

Enquiry to booking

Deposit & invoice.

The yes is fragile until the deposit lands. Make paying easy and make the next step visible.

Deposit / invoiceEmail

Great, let's lock it in. Here is the [deposit / invoice] for [project]: [link].

Once it's in we'll send the intake and we start on [date]. You'll hear from a senior person within 24 hours.

When: the instant they say yes. Pair the payment with a concrete start date.

Enquiry to booking

Payment reminder.

Gentle, then clear. Assume good faith and remove friction.

Gentle nudgeWhatsApp / Email

Quick nudge, [name]: the [invoice / deposit] for [project] is still open.

Here it is again: [link]. Let us know if anything is holding it up.

When: 2 to 3 days past due. "Anything holding it up" invites the real reason without pressure.

During the build

Kickoff.

Start with momentum. Ask for what you need to move fast, in one short list.

Welcome & startEmail

Welcome aboard, [name]. Work on [project] starts [date].

To move fast we'll need [assets / logins / content]. Short list here: [link].

Send what you have and we start with that, the rest can follow.

When: the day the deposit clears. "Start with what you have" keeps momentum instead of waiting on a full pack.

During the build

Progress update.

A calm update kills the "what is happening" worry before it starts.

Status updateWhatsApp / Email

Update on [project], [name]: [what's done] is done.

Next up: [next step], expected by [date].

Nothing needed from you right now. We'll flag the moment we do.

When: on a set rhythm (e.g. every Friday). Regular beats detailed. "Nothing needed" is reassuring.

During the build

Chase assets.

Make the ask tiny and specific, and tie it to their outcome.

Asset requestWhatsApp / Email

One thing to keep [project] moving, [name]: [the specific asset, or a review of [link]].

Whenever you have five minutes. The sooner it lands, the sooner we ship.

When: the moment you're blocked. One specific item gets actioned, a long list gets ignored.

During the build

A delay, honestly.

Get ahead of it. Own it, give a new date, show the plan. Silence is what damages trust, not a slip.

Delay noticeEmail

Straight with you, [name]: [project] will move to [new date], because [reason].

Here is what we're doing to keep the impact small: [action].

Sorry for the shuffle. You'll have [next milestone] by [date].

When: the moment you know, never after the deadline passes. A new date plus a plan rebuilds trust fast.

Delivery & beyond

Handover.

Hand over control, not just a link. Prove it works and make them look good to their team.

Go-live & handoverEmail

[Project] is live, [name]: [link]. The code, content and data are yours.

We've set up [analytics access], and we'll book a 15-minute walkthrough so your team can run it without us.

When: at go-live. Ownership plus a walkthrough is what turns a delivery into a referral.

Delivery & beyond

Ask for a review.

Ask right after a win, make it tiny, point them to the exact spot.

Review / testimonialWhatsApp / Email

Glad you're happy with [project], [name].

If it's not a hassle, a couple of lines on what changed for you would mean a lot, and it helps the next founder decide. Here is where: [link].

Two sentences is plenty.

When: right after they say they're happy. "Two sentences is plenty" removes the effort excuse.

Delivery & beyond

Thank you.

Close warm and leave the door open. A good ending is the start of the next project.

Thank youWhatsApp / Email

Thank you for trusting us with [project], [name]. It was a good build.

We're one message away whenever you need anything, and we'll check in around [timeframe] to see how it's performing.

When: a day or two after handover. The "we'll check in" line sets up the re-engagement later.

Delivery & beyond

Re-engagement.

Come back with a reason, not a nag. New result, one soft question.

Win-backWhatsApp / Email

Hi [name], it's been a while since [project / the call].

We just [new capability, or a result for a client like you]. Worth a quick look at where your numbers are now?

No pressure, only if the timing is right.

When: a few months on, or when you have a fresh result to share. The value comes first, the ask stays soft.

Delivery & beyond

Referral ask.

One name, one intro. The cheapest pipeline you will ever build, asked right after a win.

ReferralWhatsApp / Email

One favour, [name]: who is one other founder leaking enquiries the way you were before [project]?

A quick intro is the best thanks you can give, and we'll look after them the same way.

When: right after a review or a happy handover. "One name" is easy to answer.

Delivery & beyond

Reschedule.

When you have to move a call, own it and hand back two easy options.

RescheduleWhatsApp

Sorry [name], we need to move our [day] call.

Does [day] or [day] work instead? Same plan, just a new slot. Thanks for the flex.

When: as early as you can. Owning it plus two options keeps the momentum.
