# Brand Method: "Prefer to Watch?" Video Deliverable
### Nuramina Afham discovery-call film. 80 seconds, 9:16 vertical, loopable.

---

## 1. Concept

We open on a real Brand Method discovery call already in progress, then let Nuramina anchor it. She hosts, a Malaysian founder client lights up and laughs at a moment she names something they have felt for years, and her voice carries the through-line while the room does the proving. This beats a plain talking head because the claim ("your brand leaks trust before the first call") is demonstrated live: the viewer watches a real client recognise the problem and relax into the fix, which is more persuasive than one person stating it to camera. The call frame also earns the format the founder wants: warm, credible, unmistakably us. It gives a clean seam for the loop.

## 3. Shot List + Direction

### Setting (name it)
Brand Method's actual home: **MRANTI Park, Bukit Jalil**. Shoot at a real desk by a glass facade, greenery or the tech-campus blocks softly out of focus behind Nuramina. This is true, so it reads true. Add two quiet local cues on the desk: an event lanyard and a kopitiam-style kopi cup. A MacBook plus external monitor. No Starbucks, no Western skyline.

### Framing and lens feel
- **Nuramina anchor shots:** eye level, medium-close, 35mm to 50mm feel, shallow depth of field. Soft daylight from a side window, gentle fill opposite. MRANTI blurred behind.
- **Over-shoulder call shots:** shoot the laptop screen for real, showing the call grid. Do not composite a full fake Zoom window.
- **Reaction cutaways:** start slightly wide on the client, punch in on the laugh.
- **Eyeline rule:** the client always speaks to Nuramina, off-axis, never into the lens. Nuramina holds one direct-to-lens line only: the CTA at 1:04.

### Lighting
Natural window light, documentary treatment. Real skin texture, catchlights in the eyes. Do not over-grade. Avoid teal-orange cinematic looks. Slight sensor grain is welcome.

### Wardrobe
| Person | Wardrobe |
|---|---|
| Nuramina (host) | Malay-Malaysian, tudung bawal in a muted tone, plain blouse, tailored trousers. Max three colours across the outfit. Warm, composed. |
| Client (the one who lights up) | Chinese-Malaysian or Indian-Malaysian founder, ~30s to 40s, smart casual: collared shirt or fine-knit polo, sleeves rolled, no tie. Relaxed. |
| Bazil (optional cameo) | Male Malaysian, founder. Use as a brief second tile or a passing presence in the MRANTI background, smart casual. Keep him supporting, Nuramina hosts. |

### The genuine-laugh beat, and how to earn it
Roll for 20 to 30 minutes. Burn the first 5 on throwaway chat so the client relaxes. Trigger the real smile with a shared line, for example Nuramina saying "yeah, everyone's site does that." Keep rolling 2 seconds past the peak and cut on the **afterglow**: the eye-crinkle and cheek-lift as the face softens, not the wide grin. Reject any frame showing the bottom teeth in a full grin, that is the fake tell.

### How to make it loop seamlessly
- Make the resting two-shot at **0:54 to 1:04** and the **1:16 to 1:20** tail the same composition, so the film returns to where it began.
- Keep the tail motion tiny: breathing, a settling nod, a smile that holds. No camera cut on the seam.
- If generating with AI, use the matched first-frame-equals-last-frame method so motion resolves to rest.
- For the on-page ambient state, loop only a short 6 to 8 second beat (the shared laugh settling to attentive smiles). The full 80s plays on tap.

### Path B: Real shoot (lean 1-page plan, phone or mirrorless at MRANTI)

**Kit:** one mirrorless or a recent phone on a small tripod, a clip-on lav mic for Nuramina, a bounce or white board for fill, a real laptop running the call. Optional second phone as an A/B angle.

**People:** Nuramina hosting. One relaxed real client or a friendly stand-in founder on the call. Bazil optional in the background.

**Setup:** desk by a glass facade, sit Nuramina so window light hits her from the side. Dress the desk: lanyard, kopi cup, MacBook plus monitor. Frame everything **9:16** from the start.

**Run of shots (film in this order):**
1. Nuramina medium-close anchor, eye level. Three clean takes of each VO block. One take with a direct-to-lens CTA.
2. Over-shoulder of the laptop showing the live call grid.
3. Client reaction: roll long, warm the client up, trigger one real laugh, keep rolling 2s past the peak.
4. Two-shot on screen: the shared settling-laugh beat (this is your loop frame).
5. Inserts: hands on trackpad, notes being taken, the client's site scrolling on screen, kopi cup.

**Direction:** no teleprompter, no scripted line-readings for the client. Roll 20 to 30 minutes, warm up first 5, mine the middle for the honest laugh. Cut on the eye-crinkle afterglow. Client eyeline stays on Nuramina, into-lens only for her one CTA line.

**Post:** light colour grade only, keep skin texture. Burn captions per the script. Export the loop from the matched two-shot.

## 5. On-Page Treatment

How the video sits inside the existing phone mockup in the "Prefer to watch?" section.

| Element | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Resting state** | Muted ambient **loop** autoplays behind the play button: the short 6 to 8 second shared-laugh beat, `muted loop playsinline`. This gives the "alive, looping like this" feel the founder asked for without sound. |
| **Poster frame** | Frame 1 of the loop (the warm two-shot mid-smile) as the `poster`, so nothing flashes on load and the mockup looks intentional before play. |
| **Click-to-play** | Tapping the play button swaps to the full **80s** film with sound, captions on by default. Keep the existing badge `WATCH · 80 SEC` and host chip `Nuramina Afham · YOUR HOST · CCRO`. |
| **Captions** | On by default. Bold sans-serif (not Fraunces), high contrast, positioned centre-to-lower-middle so they clear the play button and the host chip. Word or short-phrase reveals, 0.3 to 0.5s each. |
| **Call-frame UI overlay** | Keep it real and minimal: a thin call-chrome bar and a small `Nuramina Afham` name tag on her tile only. A subtle connected or recording cue is fine. No cartoon full-screen Zoom mock. |
| **Mobile / data** | On mobile data, serve the poster still or the lightweight loop only. Load the full 80s on tap. Target under 2MB mobile, under 4MB desktop, 1080x1920 at 30fps, MP4 (H.264) plus WebM fallback. |
| **Loop seam** | Last frame matches frame 1 so the ambient loop returns invisibly. No hard cut. |
| **Disclosure** | If any client shown is AI-generated or composite rather than a real consenting person, add a small AI-generated disclosure for EU-facing traffic (EU AI Act Article 50, effective 2 August 2026). |
