✦The Trigger Framework

Is it time to rebrand?

Every rebrand has a root cause. If one of these sounds like your business, this is exactly what the Brand Journey is built to fix.

External triggerStrategicBrand equityPerformance
01External

Forced by corporate change

New leadership, an ownership transfer, or a company pivot forces a brand reset to match the new direction.

02Strategic

Structural realignment

Spin-offs, splits, or portfolio restructuring mean each entity needs its own distinct, standalone identity.

03External

Forced by competition

A competitor rebranded or entered your space. Staying put means being confused with them, or losing your edge.

04Strategic

Market insight & gaps

Research found an underserved niche. The rebrand repositions you to own that space before a rival does.

05Brand Equity

A change of perception

The brand reads low-quality, old-priced, or wrong-audience. A rebrand signals the business has truly changed.

06Performance

Forced by performance

Sales are slipping, leads are weak, conversion is low. The brand is failing to attract or close the right clients.

07Strategic

Product life cycle

You expanded into new products or services the current brand can no longer contain. The architecture needs an update.

08Brand Equity

More relevant & authentic

The brand feels dated or off from who you have actually become. The identity needs to catch up.

09Brand Equity

The need to simplify

A confusing message or cluttered logo. The rebrand strips it back to one clear, ownable idea that sells itself.

10Strategic

Clearer intellectual property

To trademark, license, or franchise, the brand must be distinctive enough to protect and scale legally.

11Strategic

Align with corporate strategy

New 3 to 5 year goals or investor commitments need the brand to signal ambition and credibility.

12External

Mergers & de-mergers

Two companies combining, or one splitting off, creates identity conflict. A unified or separated brand removes it.

✦Before you start

Questions before every project

The things founders always ask before they start. Answered directly, no fluff.

How does the money-back guarantee actually work?

If we miss the agreed delivery deadline, or the final delivery is materially incomplete versus the agreed scope, you get a full refund. No negotiation, no credit note. The only exceptions are delays caused by the client: late questionnaire submission or scope changes after commencement.

Why is there a RM290 entry offer? Is the quality lower?

No. The entry offer exists to remove the risk of the first transaction. The RM290 logo mark uses the same methodology and team as a RM30,000 architecture project. The difference is scope, not quality. Most clients who start at RM290 move to a larger engagement within 60 days.

How long does a typical project take?

Entry offers deliver in 7 days. Growth tier projects deliver in 2 to 4 weeks. Full brand architecture and app builds have defined timelines agreed at kickoff, typically 6 to 16 weeks. The clock starts from questionnaire submission, not from payment.

Do you work with clients outside Kuala Lumpur?

Yes. The majority of our process is fully remote by design. Kickoff, reviews, and delivery happen via WhatsApp, email, and video call. We have active clients across Peninsular Malaysia, East Malaysia, and Singapore. In-person sessions are available for Klang Valley clients on request.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer, ToyyibPay, and DuitNow QR. For projects above RM5,000 we offer a 50/30/20 split: 50% to start, 30% at the halfway milestone, 20% on final delivery. Full upfront is not required for larger projects.

What is Methodas and do I need it?

Methodas is our systems and education platform for founders who want to build without hiring an agency full-time. It gives you access to the same frameworks we use for clients. If you want it done for you, Brand Method is for you. If you want to learn and build yourself, Methodas is for you. Many clients use both.

How many revisions do I get?

Every project includes a defined number of revision rounds in the scope. Entry offers include one round. Growth and premium tiers include two to three. Revisions are based on the original brief. Direction changes after commencement are treated as scope changes and priced separately.

How is Brand Method different from Fiverr?

Fiverr delivers an asset. Brand Method delivers a system. You get a dedicated PM, a written guarantee, a defined timeline, a growth pathway, and a Malaysian team that understands the local market and SME landscape specifically. The strategy behind the asset is what separates them.
