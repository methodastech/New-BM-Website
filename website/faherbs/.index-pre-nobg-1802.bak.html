ABOUT

The factory that built Tanamera

FA Herbs began as a family cottage industry built on herbal knowledge, profiled by The Star in 2000 with founder Siti Fatimah Sheikh Salleh. Three decades on, the company runs three plants, anchors the Sedania Innovator group’s Sustainable Brands division, and remains the quiet engine behind other people’s labels: every Tanamera product is made on these lines, and that brand now sells in more than 30 countries.*

* Figure from the group’s published material, confirmed with your team before launch.
