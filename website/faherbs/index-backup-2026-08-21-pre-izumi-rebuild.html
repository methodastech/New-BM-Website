FAQ

What brand owners ask us first

The answers below are what we can state today. Anything still marked as a slot is confirmed with our team before launch.

Do you manufacture my formula, or do I use yours?
Both. Bring your own specification and we manufacture to it on our lines, or take a formula our chemists have already proven and put your brand on the pack.
What product formats can you produce?
Ten lines run in our plants today: bar soap, liquid soap, capsules and traditional medicine, creams and gels, powder sachets, essential oils, herbal tea, hand and surface sanitisers, traditional pills, and pre and post natal sets.
Are you certified?
We manufacture under GMP and Halal certification. The certificate numbers, scope and current expiry dates are published in the Proof section, and each one goes live only when our QA team supplies the current document.
Do you handle product registration?
Registration and notification paperwork is prepared alongside your label, so the pack and the filing move together rather than one waiting on the other.
Will my formula stay confidential?
Confidentiality on formulas is standard. Your specification stays yours, held batch after batch.
What is your minimum order quantity, and how long is a build?
Your MOQ per format and your realistic brief-to-delivery window print here, confirmed with your production team. We do not publish a number we cannot hold.
