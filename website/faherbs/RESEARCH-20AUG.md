## 6. Certifications B2B buyers check

| Cert | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| GMP (NPRA) | NPRA requires acceptable GMP evidence, preferably PIC/S, before product registration |
| MAL registration | Every supplement or traditional product needs a MAL number on label; N = health supplement, T = traditional medicine |
| HALAL JAKIM | Buyers building halal brands need the factory certified, not just the product |
| ISO 22716 | Cosmetics-specific GMP; the baseline skincare OEMs display |
| HACCP / MeSTI | Food safety for ingestibles; MeSTI is KKM baseline, HACCP the step above |
| SIRIM / ISO 9001 | General quality marks; FA Herbs claims ISO 9001:2008, long superseded |
