02
The strategy the site carries
Three jobs, in lead order
Job 1 · route

Send each buyer to the right website

Two websites, one factory. Website 1, ODM for startups: ready formulas, their label, fast to market, with product lines, MOQ, timeline and process shown. Website 2, OEM for established brands: custom requirements, answered with capability, R&D, certifications and capacity. The sites interlink and never dilute each other.

Job 2 · prove

Pass the brand owner's background check

A buyer choosing a contract manufacturer checks exactly what the site currently leaves blank: certificates, clients, outcomes, numbers. The proof layer exists so that check finds named certs, a named roster, 2 or 3 flagship case studies and one consistent set of figures.

Job 3 · be found

Get found by search and by AI

The meeting's stated problem: not enough prospects finding the OEM and ODM services. Chapter 07 answers it with the two keyword lanes, the schema layer and an AEO pass so the factory is findable when a founder asks ChatGPT or Gemini instead of a search box.

Engineering
Keep the CMS, rebuild the shop window, end the placeholder era
Stack

The CMS stays, the scaffolding goes

The site runs WordPress on the XStore theme. WordPress stays so the client's team keeps its editor; the XStore demo scaffolding, placeholder blocks and dead sections go. Templates are rebuilt page by page on the kept identity, which is what "refresh, not teardown" means in code. Website 1 is a fresh build with no legacy to carry.

Budget

The numbers the build must hit

Targets, set by this plan: first meaningful paint under 2.5 seconds on a phone over mobile data, images as compressed WebP real files, lazy loaded, no autoplay video. A B2B buyer forwarded a link on WhatsApp opens it on a phone; the page must not make them wait.

Hygiene

The link and claim sweep

The misspelled /portolio link and the /certificate vs /certificates split resolve with 301s into the new page set. Typos corrected estate-wide. Every certification claim restated only as evidenced, and every broken image either replaced by the shoot or removed.

One fact sheet

Facts as configuration

Client count, founding year, factory count, contacts, MOQ and timeline live in one place both websites read. One edit updates every page on both sites, which is how 100+ on one page and 250+ on another never happens again after we leave.
