        How much does HR and payroll outsourcing cost?

        PeopleX's published price for payroll with the HR app is RM80 per employee a month for 1 to 10 people, RM48 for 11 to 50 and RM40 for 51 to 100. Teams over 100 are quoted individually. Onboarding starts from RM500, paid once.

        How long does it take to switch to PeopleX?

        Most switches take 4 to 6 weeks, in 4 steps: data gathering, system setup, a parallel run and go live.

        In the parallel run we process a full payroll cycle beside your current one and reconcile both line by line. You only go live once the numbers match.

        Can we switch in the middle of the year?

        Yes. The start of a new quarter or the period after year end filing is cleanest, but a mid year switch works when your year to date payroll data is moved across correctly. We collect that data in the first step.

        Is the HR app really free?

        Yes. The HR tech platform is included free when PeopleX runs your payroll: leave, attendance, claims, shifts and employee self service.

        Who sees our salary data, and how is it kept?

        Salary information stays with a controlled PeopleX team instead of moving through internal spreadsheets and shared drives. Access is role based, logged and encrypted, and employee data is stored and processed to PDPA requirements.

        Why outsource payroll instead of running it internally?

        It suits businesses without a dedicated payroll specialist, and companies that want lower compliance risk and less internal overhead. PeopleX gives you accuracy, statutory filings on time and peace of mind.

        What should a payroll outsourcing provider in Malaysia manage?
        • EPF, SOCSO and EIS contributions at the correct rates
        • PCB (MTD) monthly tax deductions for LHDN
        • HRDF levy, for employers it applies to
        • EA forms issued to employees each year
        • Leave, working hours and overtime in line with the Employment Act 1955
        • Payslips and reports for KWSP, PERKESO and LHDN submissions
        What are the key areas of HR compliance in Malaysia?
        • The Employment Act 1955: working hours, overtime, leave and termination
        • Minimum wage under the Minimum Wages Order
        • EPF, SOCSO and EIS contributions
        • Monthly PCB (MTD) and annual EA forms
        • HRDF levy, where applicable
        • PDPA 2010 for employee records
        • The Occupational Safety and Health Act 1994
        What should a growing company look for in a payroll provider?
        • Statutory rates and rules kept up to date automatically
        • Self service for leave, claims, attendance and payslips
        • The option to outsource payroll as headcount grows
        • Employer of Record support for hiring outside Malaysia
        • Strong security for employee and salary records

        PeopleX combines managed payroll, HR administration and EOR in 1 package, so you can scale without adding admin.
