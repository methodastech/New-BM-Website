# Tenthpin MC — Design Plan: "The Control Surface"

Synthesised from four references (c2mtl·koki-kiko, ref.digital, cipherdigital, good-fella), adversarially
critiqued by an Awwwards-jury lens and a skeptical-CIO lens, then finalised. Reviewed first-hand in a live
browser.

## Concept
Tenthpin MC is a single precision instrument the CIO operates by scrolling. A dark control surface where a
visible technical grid is the literal spine of every section, and one luminous ERP-core object responds to
scroll and cursor as scattered systems converge into one governed S/4HANA backbone, with AI as a cyan signal
pulsing through it. One idea everywhere: **convergence into one engineered system, made visible.** Nothing is
decorative; everything on screen is load-bearing. Two audit rules: **if it is cyan, data is moving**, and
**every number maps to the company deck or it is cut.**

## What we steal from each reference
- **Cipher** → near-black blueprint grid with nodes + connectors, one restrained accent, auditable "trusted by" proof, engineered tone. The backbone.
- **ref.digital** → monospace reference-code index, equal-weight tile system, mask-reveal headlines, precision.
- **C2 / koki-kiko** → scroll-to-resolve hero (abstract field snaps into the mark), two-accent rationing, cutout type as a window onto motion, a persistent anchored payoff zone.
- **good-fella** → confident branded entrance, one bold claim per screen, weighted mechanical motion, credibility legible at rest.

## Art direction
- **Type:** Space Grotesk 500 display (massive, one claim per screen), Inter body (one support line per section), JetBrains Mono labels/coordinates. The extreme display-to-mono scale gap is the premium signal.
- **Colour:** warmed near-black ground (#06070B / #0A0C13 / panels #0E121C, flat). Neutrals ink #EEF2FB, soft #AEB7CC, faint #6B768E, hairlines #414B60 at ~6%. Anchor **blue #3B6FFF**. Signal **cyan #3FE0D0**, used only where data is live.
- **Space:** a real Dynamic Grid (fixed 12-col hairline overlay with mono coordinate ticks per section, e.g. `04 / AI-IN-SAP`); content snaps to columns, the active band brightens on scroll. Density pulses: giant claim → dense proof → black breathing room.
- **Texture:** engineered, near-matte. No backdrop-blur, no glow orbs, no box-shadow halos, no glassmorphism, no gradient-on-white. Light only emits from the core and live data lines.
- **Imagery:** zero stock. One generated WebGL ERP-core + data fields behind cutout headlines. Credentials as auditable readouts, not logo soup. Real client logos only once cleared (monochrome row, no carousel).

## Branding (logo kept)
- Keep the logo exactly; nav pairs it with a mono `MANAGEMENT CONSULTANTS` descriptor so the MC entity is unmistakable.
- **Real** scroll-to-resolve hero: a particle cloud of ERP nodes contracts into the lattice + logo lockup as the H1 mask-reveals.
- One signature microinteraction: a precise blue line-draw underline (mechanical, no bounce) across nav, CTA, tiles, method steps.
- A persistent anchored CTA spine ("Book a free assessment") that recurs on rest-beats and resolves in the footer finale.
- Voice: plain, outcome-first, triad cadence ("Migrate. Automate. Run."). Lead with "The SAP partner built for industry, driven by AI"; hold "Client-Centric. Insight-Driven. SAP-Focused." as a quiet anchor.

## Fact gate (enforced against CONTENT.md)
- **Removed:** Chemical (deck does not support it), "12 countries" (replaced with verified 400+ in Asia), "world-class".
- No fabricated clients/testimonials, no hard market dates, no urgency timers. Every number, cert, industry, adjective maps to the deck or is cut.

## Motion system (laws)
JS-independent credibility (hero claim/position/CTA/chips visible by default in CSS) · sub-600ms intro, no loading-counter spectacle · real scroll-to-resolve hero · scroll-as-machine (pinned, scrubbed core + grid) · genuine multi-plane parallax (data plane / display type / mono labels at three rates) · every animation encodes an idea · section 4 core reconfigures through four distinct facets with a static four-quadrant fallback · cutout-on-data headlines (sec 3 & 7) · masked clip-reveal on every headline · blue section wipes · mechanical damped easing, no bounce/elastic/magnetic · augmented cursor not cursor:none · Lenis off under reduced-motion (native scroll, pin intact) · footer finale where the core settles to rest.

## The 12 sections
0 Nav (console header) · 1 Hero (scroll-to-resolve core) · 2 Proof strip (credentials as readout) · 3 The gap (cutout problem) · 4 AI in SAP (pinned engine, 4 facets) · 5 Built for industry (Automotive, Manufacturing, Life Sciences) · 6 How we de-risk it (method as mechanism) · 7 Why now (three forces, no dates) · 8 Proof / scale (global weight, local team) · 9 The assessment (the offer) · 10 Contact (convert) · 11 Footer (designed finale).

## Reasoning (why we did it this way)
- **Art direction:** the brief's hardest words are "engineered not decorated"; the cleanest proof is to make the interface behave like the system being sold. The grid is real, the texture rules are internally consistent (no blur/glow/halo undercutting the thesis), colour is rationed to one anchor + one auditable signal, neutrals warmed to read premium not cold.
- **Branding:** the logo is kept and the system built around it; the MC descriptor and the real scroll-to-resolve protect against being mistaken for the global Life-Sciences site. The fact gate is the decisive move: one fabricated credential collapses a trust-led sale, so Chemical and "12 countries" are cut.
- **Website:** twelve sections, one job, one action, with the CTA as a real recurring spine. Section 4 is rebuilt so all four facets are distinct and a skim index serves the scanning CIO. The missing method section is added because "de-risked" must be shown. Credibility is JS-independent so the proof a CIO needs in seconds is never taxed by spectacle.
