Background

01 · Company & entity context

The SEA arm of a global SAP firm, without a home online

Tenthpin Management Consultants Sdn Bhd (TMC) is the Malaysia and SEA arm, KL and Singapore, of global consultancy Tenthpin (HQ Basel; 1,500+ consultants worldwide, 400+ across Asia, 12 countries). It is an SAP Gold Partner, also SAP SuccessFactors and Cloud qualified, ISO 9001 and 27001 certified. It is strong in Automotive, Manufacturing and Life Sciences, and its sharp edge is AI inside the ERP: RISE and GROW with S/4HANA, ECC to S/4 transformations, SuccessFactors, CRM, Clinical Trial Supply, government e-invoice. The tagline that fits is Client-Centric, Insight-Driven, SAP-Focused.

The Brand Scan
Nine lenses, plus architecture, visual, behavioural, peer and touchpoint.
03.1 · Brand architecture

One entity, endorsed by the global brand

Tenthpin MC is a single corporate brand, but it sits under global Tenthpin. The architecture only works if the MC site clearly says "part of global Tenthpin" (borrowing the 1,500-consultant credibility) while owning the broader industry-and-SAP positioning the global site has vacated.

Endorsement to keep

Backed by global Tenthpin

The site should signal the global weight, 1,500+ consultants, 12 countries, so a buyer trusts the local team can deliver at scale.

Position to own

The industry & SAP entity

Where global reads Life-Sciences-only, MC owns Manufacturing, Automotive, Chemical, Life Sciences, and AI-in-SAP, for APAC.

Architecture verdict: keep a clear "part of Tenthpin" endorsement for credibility, but give the MC site its own broader, AI-driven industrial positioning. Do not just mirror the global Life-Sciences site, that would erase the entity's reason to exist.

03.3 · Visual audit

Nothing exists yet, so the direction is everything

There is no website, palette or type system to critique, only the logo, which is sound and should be kept. That is an advantage: the single most important decision is the direction, and the direction has one job, look as advanced as the work.

The trap to avoid

The generic SI look

Stock photos of handshakes and server rooms, blue corporate gradients, jargon walls. It reads "reseller" and erases the premium AI story.

The direction

AI-driven, clean, with 3D depth

A calm, premium base punctuated by dark, dimensional showpieces where the AI and SAP story lives, layered ERP/AI stacks, node networks, real motion. Engineered, not decorated.

Type & colour

Technical, confident

A modern geometric display plus a clean body face; a confident blue anchor with restrained tech accents. The kept logo stays the constant.

The proof layer

Badges as design

SAP Gold, ISO, partner marks designed into a trust strip, not bolted on as footer clutter.

Visual verdict: the blank page is the opportunity. Approve an AI-driven, premium direction (see the Web & Brand Direction tab), hold the line against the generic SI look, and design the proof in, not on.

The Case
The argument the audit builds to, and why it matters commercially.
04 · The argument

Strong delivery, invisible brand, in a trust-led buy

An S/4HANA transformation is a multi-year, multi-million-ringgit commitment. The buyer, a CIO or transformation lead, de-risks it by choosing a partner that looks as credible as the work. Tenthpin MC has the credibility but shows none of it: no site to research, no AI proof to forward to a colleague, no industry page to send. In that gap, a better-marketed but weaker partner wins on perception alone. The corporate site is not vanity, it is the cheapest way to stop losing deals you should win.
