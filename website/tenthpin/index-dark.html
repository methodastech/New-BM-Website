It depends on your landscape and scope. The Tenthpin FAST method and a pre-built S/4HANA system shorten the runway, and your free assessment gives you a real, staged timeline rather than a guess.

RISE or GROW with SAP, which fits us?

RISE suits an existing SAP estate moving to a managed cloud transformation. GROW suits a cleaner, cloud-first adoption. We assess your starting point and recommend the path that lands fastest with the least risk.

With the IDEA-Activate approach aligned to SAP Activate: a pre-built S/4HANA system, templates and accelerators, and scaled-agile delivery in validated environments. The work is staged against clear gates, so there are fewer surprises.
