01

One page. One decision. One action.

The flagship homepage exists to do a single job: turn a CIO's visit into a booked assessment, and to win the Tuesday sign-off on direction. Everything downstream — branding, art direction, motion, copy — is in service of that one outcome.

A booked assessment

Constraint
Trust-led

The strategic insight. The substance already exists: SAP Gold Partner, ISO 9001 & 27001, 1,500+ consultants, AI inside the ERP. Until now none of it was visible online, so a better-marketed partner won on perception alone.

A multi-year, multi-million-ringgit transformation is judged on credibility a buyer can see. This site makes the substance visible — and closes the gap between how good the delivery is and how good it looks.

06

Art direction · "The Control Surface."

Tenthpin MC is a single precision instrument the CIO operates by scrolling. A calm, premium light surface, clean and trustworthy like the parent brand, with a visible technical grid as the spine of every section. It then drops into dark, dimensional instrument panels where the AI-on-S/4HANA story lives: a luminous ERP-core converging scattered systems into one governed backbone, with a cyan signal pulsing through live data. One idea everywhere: convergence into one engineered system, made visible. Nothing decorative; everything load-bearing.

Cipher

Near-black blueprint grid with nodes + connectors, one restrained accent, auditable proof. The backbone.

ref.digital

Monospace reference-code index, equal-weight tile system, mask-reveal headlines. Precision.

C2 / koki-kiko

Scroll-to-resolve hero, two-accent rationing, cutout type as a window onto motion. The entrance.

good-fella

Confident branded entrance, one bold claim per screen, weighted mechanical motion. Credibility at rest.

Space

A real 12-column hairline grid with mono coordinate ticks. Content snaps to columns; density pulses: giant claim, dense proof, open white breathing room, then a dark showpiece beat.

Texture

Engineered, not decorated. No stock photography, no cheesy blue-gradient hero, no glassmorphism, no glow orbs or shadow halos. The light base stays calm and flat; depth and glow are rationed to the dark showpiece bands, where light emits only from the core and the live data lines.

Imagery

Zero stock. One generated ERP-core behind cutout headlines. Credentials as auditable readouts. Real client logos only once cleared.

Motion laws

L1

JS-independent credibility. Hero claim, position, CTA and chips are visible in CSS by default. Proof is never taxed by spectacle.

L2

Scroll as machine. A pinned, scrubbed core and grid. Genuine multi-plane parallax: data plane, display type and mono labels move at three rates.

L3

Every animation encodes an idea. Mask-reveal on every headline, blue section wipes, the core reconfiguring through four distinct facets.

L4

Mechanical easing. Damped, weighted, no bounce, no elastic, no magnetic cursor. An augmented cursor, never cursor:none.

L5

Reduced-motion intact. Smooth-scroll off, native scroll on, pins preserved, static fallbacks for every scene.

07

The fact gate · why it is decisive.

One fabricated credential collapses a trust-led sale. So the whole site is gated against the official "TMC Company Introduction 2026" deck: every number, cert, industry and adjective maps to the deck, or it is cut. This is the discipline that lets a CIO trust the rest.

Cut — not supported
✕
Chemical industry — the deck does not support it; the claim is Automotive, Manufacturing, Life Science "and beyond".
✕
"12 countries" — replaced with the verified 400+ consultants across Asia.
✕
"World-class" and other unearned adjectives.
✕
Fabricated clients, testimonials, hard market dates, countdown timers.
Kept — verified to the deck
✓
1,500+ consultants worldwide · 400+ across Asia · KL + Singapore.
✓
SAP Gold Partner, SuccessFactors + Cloud Qualified · ISO 9001 & 27001 · AAA.
✓
95%+ long-term client relationships · part of global Tenthpin, HQ Basel.
✓
FAST + IDEA-Activate, pre-built S/4HANA, real capability: IoT, RPA, self-driving supply chains, e-invoice.
Rule 1 — if it is cyan, data is moving. The signal colour is never decorative.
Rule 2 — every number maps to the company deck, or it is cut.

Note · "driven by AI" is the recommended strategic positioning, supported by real capability. It is not yet language the company uses today — confirm comfort before publishing.
