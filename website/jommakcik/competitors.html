Competitor Audit · Brand Method

Who a family compares us to, and what each one teaches us

Jom Makcik is not compared against taxis. When an adult child needs to move a parent who cannot leave her wheelchair, the shortlist is built from anyone who says the word wheelchair, accessible, escort or companion near a Malaysian place name. Eleven operators were checked against their own live pages on 9 and 10 September 2026, every one captured, and the four with the most to teach were measured on the same throttled rig as the audit. Where a claim could not be verified it is listed in part 06 rather than promoted into a fact.

The finding that matters: in this category the ability to carry someone seated in their own wheelchair and the willingness to walk them to the door are split across different operators, and the two best funded entrants do neither. GrabAssist takes a folding chair only and its drivers may not leave the vehicle. Homage sends an escort but no vehicle. Jom Makcik is the only operator on this board that does both, and its website says neither.
00

What is confirmed, and what waits for Friday

Three things the research settles on its own. Four things only Dr Sazlina can settle.

The IAQ and Tenthpin boards open with the client's own confirmations from the review meeting. This board has not had that meeting yet. So the top of it carries what the evidence confirms without her, and the four questions that will re-rank this board once she answers them on Friday 11 September.

Confirmed · primary threat

GrabAssist is the entrant that changes the category, and it published the moat

Launched December 2025 in the Klang Valley with roughly 100 trained driver partners. Its own page limits it to a foldable wheelchair or crutches, and states that drivers are unable to leave their vehicles unattended. Those two sentences describe Jom Makcik's two products.

Source: grab.com/my/grabassist, captured 10 Sep 2026, quoted verbatim in part 03
Confirmed · the search rival

Love On Wheels is winning search on pages nobody else bothered to write

Service pages, a hospital transport page, an accessible tourism blog by state, syndicated on Medium. Its homepage headline is the search phrase itself. Weaker credentials than Jom Makcik, better findability than everyone.

Source: loveonwheels.com.my, captured 9 Sep 2026; measured FCP 3.48 s on the audit rig
Confirmed · the price anchor

Only the charities tell you what it costs

Mobiliti publishes RM17 per wheelchair user one way. Little Fook publishes RM5 plus RM1.50 per kilometre. No commercial wheelchair van operator on this board publishes a fare. The companion services publish hourly rates instead.

Source: mobiliti.org.my and shuangfu.org, captured 10 Sep 2026

Whether PERKESO and JKM are referral, funding or panel relationships. Whether Jom Makcik's own rates sit above or below Mobiliti's RM17 anchor. Whether weekend service exists in practice. Which hospitals actually refer today. Each answer moves at least one operator on the ranking below.

Real rivals, in full

The six that can take a booking Jom Makcik would otherwise win

Captured 9 and 10 September 2026. Quotes under "on their page" are read off the screenshot in part 02 or the live page text. No operator carries a rubric score here; the rank order carries the assessment, and every inference is labelled as ours.

01GrabAssistGrab Malaysia, MyTeksi Sdn Bhd and GrabCar Sdn Bhd · launched December 2025 · Klang ValleyThreat 9Site 8
On their page"GrabAssist: Travel easier with PWD-friendly rides." And in a box marked IMPORTANT: "our driver-partners are unable to leave their vehicles unattended. If you require assistance getting to the pickup point or after drop-off, we encourage you to arrange for someone to assist you."
What they sellA ride type inside the Grab app for mobility aid users, people with communication disabilities and seniors, "or anyone with foldable wheelchair or crutches", with specially trained driver partners.
VehiclesPlus range cars with larger boots. No ramp, no lift. A person who cannot transfer out of their chair cannot use it.
How they acquireThe app. Advance booking 75 minutes to 90 days ahead. Roughly 100 driver partners at launch, trained with certified care providers, in car communication flip cards.
PriceNot published. Standard Grab fare logic.
What they beat us on
  • Every family already has the app installed. There is no discovery problem.
  • Booking in under a minute, fare shown before confirming, driver tracked on a map.
  • Scale: 100 drivers on day one, in the exact areas Jom Makcik serves.
  • Bilingual by default, the app is already in Malay.
What they cannot do
  • Carry a passenger seated in a non folding wheelchair. Stated.
  • Walk anyone to a door, wait, or sit through an appointment. Stated as policy.
  • Guarantee a woman driver.
  • Speak to a dialysis household about a monthly plan.
Our read. Grab will take the ambulatory seniors and the folding chair users who want a fast pickup, and that is fine, because the margin, the loyalty and the recurring medical journeys live in the half Grab has said in writing it cannot serve. The correct response is not to compete on app convenience. It is to put Grab's two sentences on the homepage, beside a photograph of the ramp and the khaki field vest, which the prototype now does.

05

Five things the new site must do

The whole board reduced to five instructions, each already in the web plan and the prototype
Move 01

Say the two things Grab cannot do, in the hero

Stay in your own wheelchair. We walk you in. Six words the largest competitor has published that it cannot use. Placed where a comparison shopper reads first. Built.

Source: grab.com/my/grabassist capture, 10 Sep 2026
Move 02

Publish a fare, and a worked round trip

Mobiliti's RM46 round trip example is the most reassuring sentence in the set. Jom Makcik's structure is built and shows On request until Dr Sazlina confirms figures. Being the first commercial operator to publish takes the position outright.

Source: mobiliti.org.my and shuangfu.org captures
Move 03

Write the pages Love On Wheels is writing, in two languages

A page per service, a page per area, a FAQ marked up as schema, each titled with what a person types. One operator does this in English. Nobody does it in Malay.

Source: loveonwheels.com.my capture and its blog index
Move 04

Turn sixteen logos into sixteen sentences

Zero operators on this board explain a partner. Jom Makcik has PERKESO, JKM, Khazanah's foundation, four universities and two hospitals. One sentence each, on the homepage rail and a Partners page. Built, with the two agency sentences pending approval.

Source: every capture in part 02; none carries an explained partner
Move 05

Name the testimonials and match Pearlcare's operational honesty

Multicare names its reviewers. Pearlcare states 24 x 7 and the pickup mechanic. Jom Makcik's three quotes are anonymous and its hours contradict its own app. Names, towns, and one version of the hours, everywhere.

Source: multicaremobility.com and pearlcaremobility.com.my captures; audit finding 6
