Review · Brand Method

Every fault in Prototype Web 1, the fix for each, and three checks that it is fixed

The prototype was read end to end in English and in Malay, clicked through by mouse and by keyboard, scanned for accessibility, measured on the throttled rig used for the live site and the competitors, and checked against the research so that every sentence on it is either a fact the company published or a labelled gap. What follows is every fault that found, sorted three ways, with the fix that was applied and three separate checks that the fix holds. Facts only the company can supply are listed as such, not guessed.

The finding that matters: nothing on the page is broken, and the page is now four times faster than the live site on the same throttled connection. What is wrong is quieter than that: a handful of sentences that a stranger cannot follow, six partners with a logo and no sentence, internal notes that were showing as copy, and a long page with no way to jump. All of it is fixed below except the facts only the company can supply, which are listed by name.
