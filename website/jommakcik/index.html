What a standard assisted ride cannot do

These limits are not our claim. They come from the service pages the big ride hailing apps publish about their own assisted rides.

Jom Makcik

A vehicle built around the chair, and a person who stays

  • AbiliCar has a factory fitted ramp. You roll on and stay seated in your own wheelchair, the whole way.
  • A trained chaperone walks you in, waits through the appointment, and brings you home.
  • Women drivers for women, children and elders, trained with UTM and UiTM.

Roughly 600 trained women drivers in the Adinita network since 2018.

A standard assisted ride

A car with a bigger boot

  • Takes a foldable wheelchair or crutches only. You must be able to get out of your chair and into a car seat.
  • The driver is not permitted to leave the vehicle. If you need help to the door, you are told to arrange someone yourself.
  • Nobody waits with you through the appointment.

Quoted from the published service pages of the major platform operating in Klang Valley, read September 2026.

Questions families ask before they book

If yours is not here, send us a WhatsApp and a real person replies, in English or Malay.

Ask on WhatsApp
My mother cannot get out of her wheelchair. Can you still take her?

Yes. That is exactly what AbiliCar is for. The ramp comes down, she rolls on in her own chair, and the chair is secured with factory fitted restraints for the whole journey. She never has to transfer.

How much notice do you need?

Book at least 24 hours ahead where you can. For anything sooner, message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you honestly whether we can cover it.

What if the appointment runs late?

We wait. Hospital appointments overrun, and nobody should be left at an entrance because a clock ran out. Tell us at booking if a long wait is likely so we can plan the driver's day around it.

Can a family member ride along?

Yes. There is room for a companion in every vehicle, and there is no charge for one accompanying family member.

What exactly does a chaperone do?

She collects your parent from the house, helps them to the vehicle, walks them into the clinic, takes the queue number, waits through the appointment, collects the medication if needed, and brings them home. You get a message when they are back inside.

Are your drivers checked?

Yes. Adinita drivers are vetted before they join and complete driving and care training delivered with UTM and UiTM, plus programme specific training in senior and autism travel care funded by AirAsia Foundation.

Privacy notice

Jom Makcik Sdn Bhd collects the details you send us to arrange a journey: your name, your phone number, the pickup and destination addresses, the appointment time, and any mobility need you tell us about. We use them only to plan, carry out and bill the ride, and to contact you about it.

We share them with the driver and chaperone assigned to you, and with no one else. We keep booking records for as long as accounting rules require. You can ask to see, correct or delete your details by writing to admin@jommakcik.my. This notice is given under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010.
