Objections, and the honest answer

Where we agree, where we disagree, and the evidence for each

Kept in the pocket. A careful buyer raises these; a careful seller has already conceded the true ones.

The objectionThe answerOur position
Why a website at all, WhatsApp already worksWhatsApp is where the booking ends, not where a family starts. They start on Google at 4pm in a corridor. Audit part 05: the current page is not indexable for the words they type.Agree, and keep WhatsApp as the close
Grab is cheaperIt is, for someone who can get out of her chair. The qualification section says so in the company's own voice, and says who Jom Makcik is not for.Agree, in writing, on the page
Bilingual doubles the workEvery sentence on the prototype already has its BM beside it in the markup; the toggle swaps in place. The cost is her sign off on the BM copy, not a second build.Mostly no
The generated photographs are a riskThey are labelled, bounded to the scenes listed in design part 13, and scheduled for replacement by the shoot. None shows a real person claiming to be a customer.Agree, hence the shoot
Red and white is too loudThe red is the company's own, used once per screen where a person should look. Everything else is white and ink. Design part 02 measures every contrast.Disagree, with evidence
A prototype is not a siteCorrect. It is the home page at finished standard so the decisions are made on the real thing. Thirteen pages follow the same rules, plan part 03.Agree
We cannot publish pricesThen the site says On request and promises a written quote the same day, which is still more than any rival publishes. Competitors part 01.Agree, with the fallback built
