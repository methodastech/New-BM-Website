Risk Warning

Contracts for Difference ("CFDs") are complex financial products that are traded on margin. Trading CFDs carries a high risk of losing money rapidly, since leverage can work both to your advantage and disadvantage. As a result, CFDs may not be suitable for all investors because you may lose all your invested capital. Before deciding to trade, you should consider whether you understand how CFDs work and all the risks involved, by taking into account your investment objectives and level of experience, and whether you can afford to take the high risk of losing your money.
