About the group

Run from Kuala Lumpur.

We began in 2002 as Trinidad Holdings. In 2019 we became the hotel arm of MAA Group, a company listed on Bursa Malaysia, and in September 2025 we took the name Trinidad Hospitality. Today we run 8 hotels for their owners, every one under a Wyndham flag: the owner keeps the building, we run the hotel.
