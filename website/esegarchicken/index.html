Our Story
"Fresh chicken shouldn't be this hard to get."

e-segar began with a simple frustration. Melaka families and restaurants wanted fresh, certified halal chicken every day, without the early wet-market run. So we built the cold-chain, partnered with JAKIM-certified farms, and started delivering the same day it's cut.

Every bird is slaughtered at 6am, packed cold by 9am, and at your door by evening. No frozen stock. No shortcuts. Just farm-fresh halal chicken, the way it should be.
