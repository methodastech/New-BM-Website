Good Questions

What time do I need to order for same-day delivery?
+
Order before 12pm (noon) for same-day delivery. We slaughter every morning at 6am and all deliveries are completed by 6pm across Melaka.
Is the chicken really JAKIM halal certified?
+
Yes — all products carry valid JAKIM halal certification, renewed annually. The certificate number is printed on every pack so you can verify it yourself anytime.
How is the chicken kept fresh during delivery?
+
We maintain a full cold-chain at 0–4°C from slaughter to delivery. All orders are packed in insulated bags with ice packs. The chicken is never frozen — just fresh, cold, and sealed.
What areas in Melaka do you deliver to?
+
We deliver across Melaka and surrounding areas. Free delivery for orders above RM80. WhatsApp us for exact delivery charges to your area.
Can I order in bulk for restaurants or events?
+
Yes! We have B2B packages for restaurants, kedai makan, and event catering. Custom cuts and portion sizes available. Contact us via WhatsApp for bulk pricing.
What if the chicken isn't fresh when I receive it?
+
We guarantee freshness on every delivery. If your order isn't fresh, contact us immediately via WhatsApp and we'll replace it or refund you — no questions asked.
How do I place an order?
+
Order directly through this website using the cart, or WhatsApp us at +6018-4651045. Our team will confirm your order, delivery time, and total within minutes.
