The strategy the site carries
Three jobs, in revenue order
Job 1 · convince

Pass the procurement check

The buyer is a verifier by profession. The estate must hand them the licence number, the register listing, the scope sentence, the client sectors and the named team, in text, with dates, so the background check ends on our pages instead of failing there.

Job 2 · capture

Own the mandated queries

IV&V untuk projek kerajaan. RMiT testing requirements. E-invoice integration testing. Act 854 audit. Regulation manufactures these searches on a schedule, no rival answers them in BM, and the first credible estate to do so owns the lane.

Job 3 · feed

Make training the top of the funnel

Every CTFL student is a future QA lead who shortlists vendors. The academy pays its own way, warms the brand, and seeds the industry with people who learned testing from AL AiN. The lane is finished, priced and scheduled, not rebuilt.

05
The home page, scene by scene
One scroll that reads like a test report: claim, evidence, verdict

The order is the panel's own: the licence, the clients, the method, the people, the ask. Motion is restrained and reversible: this estate persuades like an institution, not a campaign. Content is visible by default; a missed animation trigger never hides a section.

1

The claim, licensed

Full-bleed hero · the register as the headline · one sentence

No stock photography. The lab's own bench or a clean abstract of it. Headline: Malaysia's independent software testing laboratory. Subline: accredited to MS ISO/IEC 17025 (SAMM 829), listed on the government's IV&V provider register. Two CTAs: request a quotation, and the government IV&V lane. The gmail banner's slot in history is a lesson, not a component.

Rule: the accreditation statement binds to the same data file as every other page. One source, no drift.

2

The numbers, finally

Counter band · reconciled figures · dates shown

Years operating, engagements delivered, certified testers, training graduates: whatever the true figures are once the client reconciles them. Each counter carries its as-at date. If a number cannot be evidenced, it does not ship; a smaller true number beats a larger doubted one.

Gate: chapter 08, row 1. QualityKiosk's counter band is the format reference.

3

The wall, in colour and in text

Client sector wall · logos restored · names written out

The Bank Islam to MARA wall returns at full strength, each logo named in text beneath it, grouped by sector: banking, government, technology, regional. Where an NDA forbids the logo, the sector row states the engagement class instead. The Datasonic and JANM case study anchors the wall.

Gate: per-name permission, chapter 08, row 3.

4

What the lab actually does

Three-lane band · lab, bench, academy · one line each

Accredited laboratory testing for the work that must stand up in an audit. Testing services and embedded QA for the work that must ship. Training for the people who do both. Each lane routes to its hub; nobody has to guess which door is theirs.

Reference: the three-lane architecture, audit chapter 08.

5

The mandate, explained before it is asked

Compliance rail · three cards that actually open

PK2.1 and what it means for a project director. RMiT 10.8 and what it means for a Head of QA. The e-invoice and Act 854 clocks. Three cards, three real pages behind them, EN and BM. This is the section that earns search traffic while the rest of the page earns trust.

The audit's job 3. Nobody on the competitor board does this.

6

The people and the paper

Team row · the certificate wall rebuilt · SSM in the open

The Lab Director's 25 years, named engineers with roles, the MOF registration, Micro Focus GOLD, HRD Corp, the SAMM certificate scanned properly and transcribed beside the image. The carousel of low-res scans becomes a documented wall a panel can cite.

Gate: bios, photos, high-res scans. Chapter 08, rows 4 and 5.

7

The ask, three doors

Split closer · calm, no urgency theatre

Request a quotation with the one-business-day reply promise. Book the academy's next intake, date shown. Or call the one number, tappable. Footer carries the licence line, the address, the SSM number, and the reconciled facts one last time.

Every journey from chapter 04 terminates here or earlier.

Copy voice for the whole estate: the lab's own register: precise, dated, unhurried. Short declaratives. Numbers with as-at dates. No superlatives without evidence: "unparalleled" and "industry's top choice" retire, "accredited, listed, since 2015" replaces them. BM written as carefully as EN.
06
Engineering
Institutional feel, campaign-grade hygiene, one data file
Stack

Static-first, CMS-editable

Hand-built pages for the lanes and the home, a light CMS surface for news, case studies and course intakes so the cadence habit survives handover. No page-builder tax: the 61-stylesheet, 75-script era ends at launch.

Budget

The numbers the build must hit

TTFB under 0.5s cached. HTML under 150KB per page. Fonts subset to the weights used, two families, not seventy-two files. First meaningful paint under 2.5s on a mid-range phone. The QA company's website passes a performance audit by construction.

Reliability

Tested like they test

Responsive verified at 375, 768 and 1440 before every release, headings as a document outline, alt text complete, tappable contact links, reduced-motion clean. The launch deliverable includes the estate's own test report, in their format, as a statement.

One data file

Facts as configuration

Licence line, register validity, counters, phone, email, address, course prices and intake dates live in one JSON the whole estate reads. When a date or figure moves, one edit updates every page, which is how the facts stay reconciled after we leave.

09
Look and feel
The lab already owns a colour. The estate learns to wear it properly
Colour

Laboratory blue, disciplined

Their royal blue survives as the single accent on a paper-white base, with a deep navy for proof bands: the gradient-everything era ends. Restraint is the design argument: the page looks like an instrument, not a brochure.

Type

Grotesk headlines, mono evidence

A confident grotesk for headlines, a working sans for body, monospace for licence numbers, dates and scope lines, so evidence looks like evidence. Two families, subset. The four-family, seventy-two-file stack retires.

Imagery

Their lab, not stock

Real benches, real screens with real dashboards, the actual certificate photographed properly, the team as themselves. The stock woman at the analytics dashboard retires. Where photography is thin, precise diagrams of the testing process beat borrowed imagery.

The modules layer

A module is a named piece of testing with a fixed scope, a fixed deliverable and one written price. It gives the lab and the bench the same thing the academy already had: something a buyer can choose and commit to without a discovery call first. The eight in the prototype were drawn from the accredited scope and the four mandates in chapter 04, so every one of them answers an obligation the buyer already carries.
