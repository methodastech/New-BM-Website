Phosphatidylserine

Human evidence: moderate

A phospholipid that is a structural building block of your brain cell membranes. It helps support normal memory and cognitive function, and has been studied for focus and attention.

Studied at 100 to 300 mg daily, over 6 to 12 weeks

KSM‑66 Ashwagandha

Human evidence: strong for stress

The most clinically studied ashwagandha root extract. It helps the body adapt to everyday stress and supports a sense of calm, with emerging evidence for focus and mental clarity on demanding days.

Studied at 300 to 600 mg daily, standardised to withanolides
03

Bacopa Monnieri

Human evidence: moderate

A traditional botanical long used for the mind and studied for memory and learning. It supports memory retention and mental clarity as part of a daily routine.
