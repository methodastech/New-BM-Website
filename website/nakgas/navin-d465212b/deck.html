About 490,000 Johor Bahru homes cook on a cylinder

Nearly every home that cooks on gas uses a cylinder. Only 21,690 homes in the whole country have piped gas.
One fixed price. A 14 kg cylinder is RM26.60, set by KPDN since 2010. The market competes on ease and service, never on the gas.
Every brand, one app. Dealers of all brands join, so the customer never has to know who sells what.

Where the figures come from

  • Gas price. RM26.60 for a 14 kg household cylinder is the KPDN controlled price, RM1.90 a kilogram, in force since 4 December 2010 (ihsanmadani.gov.my; Malaysiakini, 3 Dec 2010; The Star, 8 Apr 2026). Household cylinders were unchanged in the April 2026 commercial increase.
  • Delivered price. RM30.00 all in for 14 kg in Johor Bahru, RM26.60 gas plus RM3.40 delivery: Navin, call of 1 October 2026.
  • Households. Johor Bahru district 460,700 at the 2020 census (DOSM); about 490,000 in 2026 is Brand Method's arithmetic at the 2020 ratio. Johor 1,130,600 in 2024 (OpenDOSM). Malaysia 8.2 million (DOSM, Census 2020).
  • Piped gas. 21,690 residential customers nationwide at 31 December 2025 (Gas Malaysia Annual Report 2025).
  • Rivals. Read 13 October 2026 on each rival's own site, store listing, code and public data feed. The Gas PETRONAS Home Delivery app ended 31 January 2023 (TechTRP, 17 Jan 2023).
  • Market in orders and scenarios. Brand Method arithmetic: households times 6 to 12 cylinders a year, times RM33.50 or RM3.50; scenarios use 9 a year and RM2.83 kept per online order. The buying rate is an assumption to validate.
  • The bill and the fee. Option B, RM3.50 per order, is Navin's leaning on the call of 1 October 2026; the gateway at about 2% is his figure for Razorpay Curlec, to confirm.
  • The engine. 45 seconds and nearest first: Navin, 28 September 2026. Four declines to Admin: the scope of work. The map on slide 5 is OpenStreetMap data of Taman Molek.
  • Fitting. Domestic regulators in Malaysia are clip-on low pressure types (MS 1165); the cylinder stays upright (Energy Commission guidance).
  • The build and the round. RM48,000 before SST, five weeks: Brand Method proposal of 29 September 2026. First round of RM60,000: call of 1 October 2026; the split of the RM8,160 is to confirm with Navin. Cradle CIP Spark: cradle.com.my, read 6 October 2026. App screens carry sample data.
