Who a household compares NakGas to

Every way a Malaysian home gets a cooking gas cylinder today, first read on its own site, store listing or code on Tuesday 6 October 2026 and read again in full on Tuesday 13 October. This page is also the price comparison audit and the competitor checkout check Bazil promised Navin on 1 October: for one 14 kg cylinder, the lines each rival shows, and the total where one is published.

The real rival

A phone number on a sticker

RM30.00 all in for 14 kg in Johor Bahru, no app, no fee. Every app has to be worth more than that call.

The field, 13 Oct

Klang Valley start-ups

Click MyGas is live in six Klang Valley areas with 23 suppliers. Gasbee says it is testing. No multi-brand app serves Johor; ACGAS, one brand, lists JB.

The surprise

The same model is live

GasXpress, registered 28 Aug 2026, routes orders to licensed dealers. It has 4, all in the Klang Valley, each covering 2 to 5 km.

Our read

Option B, one line, RM3.50

No live rival publishes an all-in total for one cylinder. The comparison that matters is RM33.50 against the RM30.00 phone call.

What changed

Every rival re-read on Tue 13 Oct 2026, on its own site, store listing, code or public data feed. Seven things move the picture.

  1. No multi-brand app serves Johor Bahru. Play Store and App Store searches in English and Malay, and the dealer feeds of the two dealer-model rivals, found no app that delivers every brand in JB. The nearest GasXpress dealer is 290 km away (its own dealer feed, queried from a JB point).
  2. Gas PETRONAS Home Delivery is a phone number now. Its "Order Now" button dials 1300 88 8427. The GPHD app ended on 31 Jan 2023 (TechTRP, 17 Jan 2023) and is in neither store.
  3. Click MyGas went live. "Launching soon" on 6 Oct; on 13 Oct it says it delivers now in six Klang Valley areas. Its public coverage feed lists 23 dealer suppliers and 15 freelance riders, 22 riders active.
  4. Gasbee says it is testing. One read of its site code on 13 Oct found deliveries paused until testing is complete; a second read the same day did not find the notice, so its status is unverified. The Play listing takes payment: 3.5 stars from 8 ratings, 500+ installs.
  5. A brand-owned app now lists Johor Bahru. ACGAS (Astana City Group) released an iOS ordering app on 25 Nov 2025; its dealer page lists Johor Bahru, Pasir Gudang, Tebrau, Iskandar Puteri and Kulai as operational. One brand, composite cylinders, 1K+ Android installs, prices not shown.
  6. The dealer-model rival is small. GasXpress lists 4 dealers, all in the Klang Valley, each delivering 2 to 5 km. Its admin defaults would put one landed 14 kg at RM43.10 if they are live.
  7. No cylinder brand has an ordering app. PETRONAS, Petron, Solar Gas, BHPetrogas, MiraGas and MyGaz all sell home delivery through a hotline and a dealer list. Their apps, where they exist, are loyalty apps.
Our read. NakGas’s opening is wider than it looked on 6 Oct: the incumbent closed its app, the multi-brand app rivals are Klang Valley start-ups with a handful of suppliers each, and Johor Bahru still orders by phone. ACGAS is the one to watch in JB: a single brand, but licensed, with an app and a celebrity budget.

The real rival

The dealer's phone number, on a sticker on the cylinder or saved in WhatsApp.

Fact · Navin, 1 Oct 2026
RM30.00 all in, by phone, Johor Bahru

RM26.60 for the gas at the KPDN controlled price plus RM3.40 for delivery. No app, no platform fee, cash at the door.

Fact · found 1 Oct 2026

Johor Bahru dealers who deliver

New Town Trading (Ulu Tiram), Guna Trading (since 1981), Uncle Lim Gas Delivery (near Gelang Patah). None publishes a delivery tariff. Gas PETRONAS Home Delivery covers Johor Bahru and 35 other Johor towns by hotline (read 1 Oct).

What the call does well
  • A person the household knows, often for years
  • No app, no sign-up, no card: cash at the door
  • Fast inside his own patch, and he knows the road and the stairs
  • The cheapest all-in figure we have: RM30.00
What the call does badly
  • Find the number first: the sticker peels, the chat scrolls away, a new home has none
  • Call around when he is closed, out of stock or not answering
  • No price shown before he arrives; no receipt, no photo
  • No warning before the gas runs out mid-cook
Fact, a dealer's own problem. A 2022 project at Universiti Tun Hussein Onn Malaysia (Batu Pahat, Johor) built an ordering and delivery app for an LPG dealer, Chuan Huat LPG Sdn. Bhd., because customers who phoned in an order did not know when it would arrive when staff were busy or stock ran out (AITCS Vol. 3 No. 1, online 31 May 2022).
Our read. NakGas does not replace the dealer. It replaces the sticker: the same licensed dealer, found without a number, at the law's price, with a photo at the door and a reminder before the flame goes yellow. The RM3.00 or RM3.50 on top has to buy exactly that, every time.

The phone row assumes the dealer charges RM3.40 per cylinder, as the brief's two cylinder example does. Our read Because the fee is per order, the gap to the phone call narrows per cylinder: RM3.50 on one, RM1.75 a cylinder on two.

Our read on the fee: option B, one line, RM3.50.
  1. One line, not two. Gasbee shows a service fee and a processing fee; GasXpress can show both. "Platform & processing fee" says the same in one line, next to the KPDN price the law wants shown.
  2. The extra 50 sen pays for the gateway. About RM2.83 kept against about RM2.34: about 21% more per order, for RM0.50 the customer barely sees.
  3. The total stays well clear of Navin's ceiling. RM33.50 is RM5.50 to RM6.50 under RM39 to RM40.
  4. No live rival undercuts it in public. No live service publishes an all-in total for one cylinder. The only published totals are retired. The comparison a household actually makes is RM33.50 against the RM30.00 call.
  5. Launch hook. Click MyGas promises free delivery on the first 3 orders. NakGas cannot waive the dealer's delivery fee, but it can waive its own platform fee on a household's first 3 orders. The cost is the RM3.50 not earned, plus about 60 sen of gateway on each of those online orders.
Option A would be the better call if the first Johor dealers' delivery fees come in well above RM3.40, or if Navin wants "RM3 fee" as a headline. Either way the decision is Navin's; the figure stays editable in Admin.

Copy and avoid

The lessons for the build, each from something a rival does or did. Our read

Copy

  • Click MyGas’s plain promise and the KPDN price named on the page
  • GasXpress’s reading of the rules: three cylinders a household, swap charges priced up front, refunds in plain Malay
  • BeliGas’s honesty about the high-rise surcharge, stated before the door
  • Gas PETRONAS’s chosen date and slot for scheduled orders
  • Gas2u’s idea of predicting the run-out, done first from the customer’s own rhythm

Avoid

  • Taking payment while deliveries are paused (Gasbee)
  • Claims the installs cannot carry (Gasbee’s 10K+ orders against 500+ installs)
  • Four fee lines that end above the phone price (GasXpress defaults, RM43.10)
  • Cashless only, which turns away households and dealers who use cash (Click MyGas)
  • A launch with no stated coverage, which leaves customers waiting on an order nobody can take (Gas2u, Gasbee)
  • Closing the app before it has users; build the dealer side first so the first orders land (GPHD)
