What happens after Send

  1. The application lands in Admin › Approvals as New, source Website, with the time.
  2. An admin checks the KPDN licence: number, expiry, and that the name matches SSM.
  3. Approved: the dealer gets an SMS invite to the supplier app, logs in with the same mobile number, and the record moves to Suppliers.
  4. In the app the dealer adds the bank account, drivers and trucks, stock and the delivery radius, then can go online.
  5. Rejected: the reason is recorded and sent to the dealer.

Not settled

  • How the team is told a new application has arrived. Owner: Navin.
  • Whether KPDN offers a licence lookup or the check stays by document. Owner: Navin.
  • Our read A duplicate check on the mobile number and the SSM number stops one dealer applying twice.
  • Our read Bank details stay inside the supplier app, after approval, rather than on a public web form.

Soalan lazimFAQ

Soalan yang selalu ditanya.Questions people ask.

Berapakah harga gas?How much is the gas?

Tong gas LPG 14 kg dijual pada harga kawalan KPDN, RM26.60 (RM1.90 sekilogram). Caj penghantaran dan fi platform ditunjukkan sebagai baris berasingan sebelum anda membayar.

A 14 kg LPG cylinder is sold at the KPDN controlled price, RM26.60 (RM1.90 a kilo). The delivery fee and the platform fee are shown as separate lines before you pay.

Siapakah yang menghantar gas?Who delivers the gas?

Pengedar gas berlesen KPDN di kawasan anda, yang memang menghantar gas setiap hari. Anda akan nampak nama pengedar dan nombor plat lori.

A KPDN licensed dealer in your area, the ones already delivering gas every day. You see the dealer’s name and the truck plate.

Boleh bayar tunai?Can I pay cash?

Boleh. Bayar tunai semasa gas sampai, atau bayar dalam talian melalui FPX, kad atau e-dompet.

Yes. Pay cash when the gas arrives, or pay online by FPX, card or e-wallet.

Saya tinggal di bangunan tinggi. Boleh hantar?I live in a high-rise. Can you deliver?

Boleh. Simpan alamat anda bersama tingkat, lif dan nombor unit, supaya pengedar tahu sebelum sampai. Caj penghantaran untuk bangunan tinggi mungkin berbeza.

Yes. Save your address with the floor, lift and unit, so the dealer knows before arriving. The delivery fee for a high-rise may differ.

Boleh tukar tong kosong?Can I swap my empty cylinder?

Boleh. Pilih Tukar tong, dan pengedar akan mengambil tong kosong anda. Jika belum ada tong, pilih Tong baharu; harganya ditunjukkan sebelum anda membayar.

Yes. Choose Swap and the dealer takes your empty cylinder. No cylinder yet: choose New cylinder, and its price is shown before you pay.

Bagaimana jika tiada pengedar yang menerima?What if no dealer accepts?

Pesanan anda dihantar kepada pengedar terdekat yang seterusnya. Jika empat pengedar menolak, pasukan kami mengambil alih pesanan anda dan menguruskannya sendiri.

Your order goes to the next nearest dealer. If four dealers decline, our team takes over your order and handles it directly.
