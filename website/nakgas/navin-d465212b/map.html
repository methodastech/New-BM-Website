The name: NakGas

Set by Brand Method on Tue 20 Oct on Bazil’s instruction: “change the name all to NakGas”. It replaces Gaslah, our name of Mon 12 Oct, and Gas Sekarang, our name of Tue 6 Oct. To confirm with Navin

One word in copy, “NakGas”; lowercase in the wordmark, the handles and the file names
Why
  • “Nak gas” is what a household says the moment the cylinder runs out: I want gas, we need gas. The name is the moment of need; the app is the answer.
  • “Nak” is the most everyday Malay word for want. Six letters, two syllables, very Malaysian: the “something very Malaysian” Navin asked for on 1 Oct. Our read
  • The flame idea is unchanged: api biru, the healthy flame our mothers read. Since 20 Oct the flame is green with that blue variation kept as its core, so the story holds. Our read
  • GasNow is weak to own: gasnow.com has been registered since 2010 and “Gas Now” is an app name abroad (audit finding F15).
  • How it got here: on 1 Oct Navin offered what the notes wrote as “Gas Karang”. In Malay “karang” can mean “later”, so we read it as Gas Sekarang, the Malay for Gas Now, and chose that on 6 Oct. On 12 Oct Bazil asked for a shorter, more memorable name and Brand Method chose Gaslah. On 20 Oct Bazil wrote “change the name all to NakGas”. Our read
Rejected or replaced on the way, 6, 12 and 20 Oct

MaiGas12 Oct: sounds the same as MyGas and MyGaz; maigas.com.my is Maigas Engineering, a Malaysian engineering company

Checks
  • Done nakgas.my and nakgas.com.my shown available by MYNIC RDAP, 20 Oct
  • Done nakgas.com not registered: Verisign RDAP 404, whois no match, 20 Oct
  • Done App Store Malaysia (iTunes search API), 20 Oct: “nakgas” returns no app; “nak gas” returns one unrelated game
  • Done Google Play Malaysia, 20 Oct: no app named NakGas in the search results
  • Open MyIPO trademark search
  • Open Social media handles
  • Open Navin’s yes
  • Done Shorter than "sekarang", checked 9 Oct on Bazil's question: Gas Kini (gaskini.com registered since 2018), Gas Terus (gasterus.com registered 2021 and an app called GasTerus on the Malaysian App Store), Gas Sini (gassini.com, 2010), Gas Cepat (gascepat.com, registered 3 Oct 2025). Free on .com: Gas Segera, Gas Laju, Gas Kilat, Gas Jom, Jom Gas. Our read then: keep Gas Sekarang; the stacked lockup answers the length, and the word carries the promise. Superseded on 12 Oct by Gaslah, and on 20 Oct by NakGas
