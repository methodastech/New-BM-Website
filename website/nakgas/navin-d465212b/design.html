      The design system, light

      One flame, one green with a blue variation, two typefaces, sixteen icons and the parts the three products are built from. Every value on this page is a token in assets/brand.css: change it there, never in a screen. The reasons behind the brand are on Brand Creation; the brand on one page is on Final Brand.

      What the name means

      NakGas: “nak gas”, what a household says the moment the cylinder runs out. I want gas. The green flame is the proof that the gas is good. Each word gives the design one job. Name and colour set on Tue 20 Oct 2026.

      WordMeaningWhat it gives the design
      nakThe most everyday Malay word for want: the moment of needUrgency you can see: the order button first on every screen, short flows, times on every status
      gasThe product, LPG, and the answer to the needThe honest bill: the KPDN price first, one fee, nothing hidden
      The green flameThe flame of a clean burn: green as the one accent, the blue variation our mothers read kept inside itThe mark, and the one colour that means gas

      What the meaning asks of the design

      Six asks. Each is answered by one part of the system, and only one.

      “Nak gas” asks for speed you can see

      Answered by the order status steps and the 45 second countdown: the customer and the dealer both see time moving.

      Components
      “Fair” asks for an honest bill

      Answered by the itemised bill: the KPDN price on the first line, every fee named, figures aligned.

      The bill
      “The flame” asks for one colour that means gas

      Answered by flame green as the only accent: the logo, the primary button, the active state. About 5% of a screen. The blue stays only as the variation set; the inner cone is mint #8FDDB8.

      Colour
      “Your dealer” asks for a person, not a fleet

      Answered by the dealer card: a name, a truck plate, the KPDN check. Never a phone number.

      Dealer card
      “Malaysian” asks for type that sets Malay well

      Answered by Plus Jakarta Sans for display and Inter for the interface, both tested on long Malay words.

      Type
      “Calm in the kitchen” asks for quiet screens

      Answered by white and base, hairlines instead of boxes, and colour only for status signals.

      The logo

      A green flame with the blue inner cone of a clean burn. Two paths, two colours, drawn on a 64 unit grid; green with the blue variation since 20 Oct 2026. The files are in Final Brand, Files.

      20 Oct: the flame was redrawn (a fuller flame with a curling tip and a small left tongue, the blue variation as a teardrop at the base) because the 12 Oct mark read as a water drop, and the wordmark was tried in Rubik and Unbounded before Outfit 800 was kept. By evening Zieel asked for the first logo back ("the same logo like the first time"), so the teardrop flame with its inner cone and the Outfit wordmark returned, in green: emerald with a mint cone, no blue. Every file in assets/ and every inline copy was regenerated from one source (tools/flame-source.svg, tools/wordmark.py); the 12 Oct drop and the Outfit outlines stay in .bak-20261020/. Chosen from a sheet of eight faces, three flames and five colour sets (tools/logo-20oct.html).

      1. The tip leans 5.5 units right of the centre axis, 3.5 units from the top: a flame bends, it never stands square.
      2. One tongue. The left shoulder swells and sweeps up to the tip in one stroke: no second tongue, no notch, so the mark stays whole at 16 px and on a tile.
      3. The inner cone is the flame itself at 40 percent, 15 by 22 units, centred on the axis and seated 4.5 units above the foot: the blue variation of a clean burn, the proof that the gas is good. The inner cone is mint #8FDDB8 (--gs-core); the all-blue variation set (assets/*-blue.svg) keeps the first blue pair, #1550F5 with #8DB0FF.
      4. The round base sits 5.5 units above the grid’s foot, like a flame on its burner.
      5. The box is 37.5 by 55 units, centred on the axis. Every lockup aligns to this box, never to the 64 unit tile.

      Clear space and minimum sizes

      The unit is C, the width of the inner cone: 15 units, a little over a quarter of the flame’s height. Keep at least one C clear on every side of any logo.

      Ratio on a screen

      White and base 60% Ink and greys 25% Wash 10% 5%

      About 60% white and base, 25% ink and greys, 10% wash, 5% flame green, and one accent-filled element per screen. Why green with a blue variation, decided 20 Oct 2026: Bazil asked for a green flame with the blue kept (“use green flame colour keep the blue, as variation”, “can be a combination but if green is better go ahead”). Pure green in Malaysia collides with Grab (#00B14F, delivery) and PETRONAS (#00A19C, gas), so ours is a deeper, bluer green, and the blue variation keeps the clean-burn story the app and the website copy are built on. The market’s other colours stay taken: yellow for Gasbee, MyGaz and MiraGas, red for Petros. The all-blue set (flame #1550F5) is kept as a variation. Our read

      Type

      Two families in the product, each with one job, and the wordmark drawn once as outlines in Outfit 800 (since 9 Oct; Rubik and Unbounded were tried on 20 Oct and dropped: its single-storey g and round a sit with the flame; the file is paths, so no page needs the font). Plus Jakarta Sans for display: designed in Jakarta, it reads naturally in Malay. Inter for everything read in the interface. No third family, and no typewriter or code fonts.

      Display
      Gas sampai.

      Do and don’t

      The rules a developer or a new designer breaks first.

      Do

      • One accent-filled element per screen: usually the primary button.
      • Put the KPDN price on the first line of every bill and receipt.
      • Icons from the duotone set only, ink or text-safe green; in rows on the wash tile, never a third colour.
      • Use 14 px radius on cards and 10 px on buttons, inputs and chips; documents stay square.
      • Space on 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32 and 40 only; 16 px screen gutter.
      • Check every screen at 300 px wide in both languages.

      Don’t

      • Fill a field behind a paragraph with flame green, or use it as a gradient.
      • Use the blue variation, the flame green or the amber fill colour for small text.
      • Put an icon in a tinted tile, or add shadows other than the tab bar’s hairline.
      • Mix pill shapes with 14 px cards on one screen.
      • Show the dealer’s phone number anywhere in the customer app.
      • Add a mascot, 3D, glass or illustration to the interface.
