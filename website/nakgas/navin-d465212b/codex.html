    For everyone. What NakGas does, in plain words.

    NakGas is the gas dealer you already trust, now in your phone. You order a cylinder in two taps, the nearest licensed dealer accepts within 45 seconds, the price the government sets is the first line of your bill, and you never call around again. No app words needed.

    A month with NakGas

    Aisyah lives in Taman Molek, Johor Bahru, in a landed house, and cooks every evening. This is one ordinary month, told with the prototype's own sample order.

    1. Day 1, 6:40pmThe flame is low. She opens the app, picks Bahasa Malaysia, types her number, enters the six digit code, saves the address. Order gas: swap, 14 kg, one cylinder, now.
    2. 6:41pmThe bill before she pays: RM26.60 the controlled price, RM3.40 delivery, RM3.50 one platform fee. RM33.50, cash at the door.
    3. 6:41pmThe nearest dealer with stock has 45 seconds. Kedai Gas Seri Molek accepts in six. She sees the dealer's name and the truck plate, never a phone number.
    4. 7:25pmConfirmed, dealer assigned, out for delivery, delivered. The driver photographs the cylinder at her gate; the photo and the receipt stay in her order.
    5. Day 2Four stars and a tag, "on time". The next reminder is set from her own rhythm, about 28 days.
    6. Day 26"Time to reorder?" lands on the lock screen. Day 27: Reorder, then Pay. Two taps. The same dealer is the nearest again.

    Questions customers ask, answered

    Who delivers my gas?

    The KPDN licensed dealer nearest you with stock. You see the dealer's name and the truck plate, never a personal phone number.

    What does it cost?

    The gas is the KPDN controlled price, RM26.60 for a 14 kg cylinder. The delivery fee is set by the dealer and shown first. One platform fee is charged per order, not per cylinder. All of it before you pay.

    Can I pay cash?

    Yes. Cash on delivery, or FPX, card and e-wallet in the app.

    I live in a high-rise. What changes?

    Your address stores the floor, the lift and the unit, so the driver knows before setting off. Any extra charge is the dealer's and shown before you pay. The tiers are still to confirm.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes, before the dealer sets off. The cancellation rules sit in Profile.

    Is it in Bahasa Malaysia?

    Yes. You choose Bahasa Malaysia or English on the first screen, and can change it in Settings. The dealer app has both; the admin portal is in English.

    What about safety?

    Safety tips sit in Profile and on Home. A healthy flame burns blue; the smart regulator that reads the gas balance is Phase Two, not in this build.

    For professionals. How dealers, regulators and investors read it.

    A dealer, a KPDN officer, an investor or the developer does not ask what the app looks like. They ask three questions, in this order, and place a delivery platform by its answers.

    1. 1Is the bill legal?Price

      KPDN requires the lines unbundled. Every bill prints the controlled price first (RM26.60 for 14 kg, set 8 Apr 2026), then the dealer's delivery fee, then one platform and processing fee per order. Nothing is folded into the gas price, and the customer sees all three before paying. The fee itself, RM3.00 or RM3.50, waits on the price audit.

    2. 2Who carries the money?Model

      No commission: the gas price and the delivery fee are the dealer's in full. The platform fee is the platform's, once per order. Online payment runs through one Malaysian gateway on the company's account, about 2 percent on the highest channel, about 67 sen on RM33.50. Cash orders are marked collected by the driver and the fee is netted against the dealer's wallet: our read, to confirm. Payouts weekly, a sample cadence.

    3. 3Will dealers accept?Supply

      The order goes to the nearest available shop or truck with stock, with 45 seconds to accept. A decline or a timeout moves it to the next nearest; four in a row escalate to an admin who assigns by hand. Dealers join with a KPDN licence, SSM and a bank account; the licence expiry is tracked and flagged, and an expired licence receives no offers. Seasoned drivers who will not use an app are the known risk: the messaging fallback is a Phase Two option.

    The order path, as the engine runs it

    Order

    Swap or new, size, quantity, now or a slot. The bill shown before payment.

    Offer

    The nearest available dealer with stock gets 45 seconds. Location is read only while the dealer is online.

    Accept

    Accept, or decline and the next nearest is offered. Four declines in a row: an admin assigns.

    Deliver

    Status pings from the driver's confirmations: confirmed, assigned, out for delivery, delivered. No live GPS for the customer in Phase One.

    Prove

    A photo at the door, mandatory. Cash marked collected. The receipt to the customer.

    Settle

    The wallet: gas and delivery to the dealer, the fee to the platform, cash orders netted. Weekly payouts approved in admin.

      Background

      The lane to own, the reading rules this page follows, every term placed by layer, and what is applied and what comes next.

      The lane to own

      Own this now
      • The household cylinder, 14 kg, swap or new, in one district at a time: Johor Bahru first.
      • The licensed dealers already on the road, with no commission, so supply says yes.
      • The controlled price printed first, as the law requires, said plainly wherever a customer decides.
      • The reminder: never caught mid-cook, first by rhythm, later by the regulator.
      Resist this
      • Everything for everyone: commercial C50, hardware sales, every state at once.
      • Competing with a brand's own app on price: the price is the law's, the service is ours.
      • A live map, a loyalty programme and a smart device before the first thousand orders.
      • Drivers forced into an app they will not open: watch adoption, keep the fallback ready.
      Source Brand Creation tab (positioning) · Competitors tab (where NakGas wins) · the audit, Verdict

      The reading rules

      • Plain words first. The meaning, then the number, then the unit.
      • Every short form written out. On first use, with the short form after it.
      • Facts are dated, reads are marked as reads. The controlled price is a fact; a fee option is a read until the audit.
      • Nothing invented. A fact not in the briefs is written "to confirm", never guessed.

      The terms, placed by layer

      Gas

      LPG
      Liquefied petroleum gas: the cooking gas in the cylinder.
      14 kg
      The household cylinder, the hero of Phase One. 12 kg depends on the brand; C50 is commercial, Phase Two.
      Swap, new
      Swap: hand over the empty, pay for the gas. New: a first cylinder, its price set by the dealer and added at checkout.
      Controlled price
      RM1.90 a kilo, RM26.60 for 14 kg, set by KPDN on 8 Apr 2026, Peninsular, confirmed for Sabah and Sarawak in the audit.
      Regulator
      The valve on the cylinder. The smart one, Phase Two, reads the balance.
      Api biru
      The blue flame: a healthy burn, the brand's idea, kept since 20 Oct as the blue variation inside the green mark.

      Rules and money

      KPDN
      Kementerian Perdagangan Dalam Negeri dan Kos Sara Hidup: licenses dealers, sets the controlled price, requires unbundled bills.
      SSM
      Suruhanjaya Syarikat Malaysia: the business registration a dealer shows at onboarding.
      PDPA
      Personal Data Protection Act 2010: consent before details are kept, on the form and in both apps.
      FPX, e-wallet, COD
      Online banking, a wallet app, or cash on delivery: the three ways to pay, through one Malaysian gateway or at the door.
      Platform fee
      The one line per order that is the platform's: RM3.00 or RM3.50, pending the price audit.
      SST, GMV
      Sales and service tax, 8 percent on the build price. Gross merchandise value: everything sold through the platform, on the admin dashboard.

      Build and operations

      Phase One
      The five week build: three products, the website, the order engine, the heat map.
      Phase Two
      Not priced: the smart regulator, live map, messaging fallback, C50, Chinese and Tamil.
      Week 1
      The Monday after the deposit clears. No calendar date exists until then.
      OTP
      One-time password: the six digit code by SMS that logs a customer or a dealer in. No password.
      Radius, escalation
      How far a dealer delivers; what happens after four declines in a row: an admin assigns.
      Settlement
      The weekly payout to dealers from the wallet, cash orders netted, approved in admin.
