The NakGas brand

One flame, one name, one green with a blue variation. This page holds the brand as it stands on Tue 20 Oct 2026, and nothing else. The reasons are on Brand Creation, the system on Design. Three choices for Navin are near the end.

Logo

2

Name

NakGas: “nak gas” is what a household says the moment the cylinder runs out. I want gas, we need gas. The name is the moment of need, and the app is the answer. “Nak” is the most everyday Malay word for want. Six letters, two syllables, very Malaysian. Brand Method set it on 20 Oct 2026 on Bazil’s instruction (“change the name all to NakGas”), after Gas Sekarang on 6 Oct and Gaslah on 12 Oct; it is still to confirm with Navin. The wordmark is lowercase “nakgas”, drawn as outlines in Outfit ExtraBold (since 9 Oct; Rubik and Unbounded tried on 20 Oct and dropped): “nak” in ink #0B1530, “gas” in flame green #0CA66A.

7

Rules

Ten rules that keep the brand the same everywhere.

  1. 1
    Use the files. Never redraw the flame or retype the wordmark.
  2. 2
    One accent. Flame green marks the logo, the primary button and the active state: about 5% of a screen. As small text it is #08854C; on a green fill the text is ink.
  3. 3
    The blue variation stays in the mark. In the green set #1550F5 is never text, never a fill, never a background.
  4. 4
    Give it air. One C clear on every side, where C is the width of the inner cone.
  5. 5
    Mind the size. Mark at least 16 px tall, horizontal logo at least 24 px. Below 16 px, the favicon tile.
  6. 6
    White on colour. On flame green or ink, use the white files. Never the green flame on green.
  7. 7
    Write the name right. NakGas in copy, one word with a capital N and a capital G; nakgas, lowercase, in the wordmark, the handles and the file names.
  8. 8
    Signals are signals. Amber, green and red speak about an order, never decorate. Amber is never text.
  9. 9
    Price first. The KPDN controlled price is the first line of every bill, receipt and price table.
  10. 10
    Keep it flat. No mascots, gradients, shadows, 3D, outlines or tilts. Never stretch, rotate or recolour the flame.

Three choices for Navin

Everything above shows our recommendation. A no on any one changes only what is listed under it.

1. The name: NakGas

“Nak gas”, what a household says the moment the cylinder runs out: the moment of need in six letters, and the “something very Malaysian” he asked for on 1 Oct. nakgas.my, nakgas.com.my and nakgas.com are free (20 Oct), and no app of that name is on the Malaysian App Store or Google Play.

If no: GasNow stays, or Gaslah (12 Oct) or Gas Sekarang (6 Oct) comes back. The wordmark, the app name, the store listing and the handles change; the flame, the colours and the type stay. gasnow.com has been taken since 2010 and “Gas Now” is an app name abroad, so the domain becomes a .my to check.

2. The colour: flame green #0CA66A with the blue variation #1550F5

A clean LPG flame burns blue, and that blue stays as a variation set; our mark is the first flame in green, emerald with a mint inner cone, clear of the yellow, red and teal of the cylinders, and deeper than Grab or PETRONAS green. Decided 20 Oct 2026.

If no: the all-blue set of 6 Oct is ready as the variation: ten *-blue.svg files in assets/ and four tokens in brand.css (flame #1550F5, text-safe #0F3CC4, core #8DB0FF, wash #EEF3FF). Our caution: a green flame alone is not a clean burn, which is why the blue variation stays in every green mark.

3. The app icon: white flame with the blue variation on green

No letters, so it reads at 29 px; the blue variation carries the idea onto the home screen.

If no: the alternatives are app-icon-blue.svg, the all-blue tile, or the green flame on a white tile. One file and the store images change. Our caution: a white tile disappears on a light home screen.
