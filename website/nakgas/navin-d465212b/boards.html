Customer journey: first open to reorder

Fifteen steps from the first open to the reorder about a month later. Read in number order. Every step opens the screen that shows it in the prototype.

1Open the appThe flame and the promise: "Gas sampai. Tak payah call."
2Pick a languageBahasa Malaysia or English on first open; switch any time in Settings.
3Phone and OTPA phone number and a six digit code by SMS. No password.
4Add an addressLanded or high-rise. High-rise adds the unit, floor and lift.
5HomeThe usual order, the reorder reminder and one Order button.
6Choose the gasSwap or new cylinder, 14 kg, brand, quantity.
7Now or scheduledNow goes straight to the order engine; scheduled picks a day and a slot.
8CheckoutThe KPDN controlled price first, then delivery, then one platform and processing fee per order.
9PayFPX, card, e-wallet or cash on delivery. One promo or points per order.
10Dealer assignedThe engine offers the order to the nearest dealer. The customer never picks.
11Out for deliveryThe dealer's name and truck plate, never a phone number. Status from driver confirmations.
12DeliveredPhoto proof taken at the door, and the receipt.
13RateStars, quick tags and an optional comment.
14Reminder, about 28 days laterPhase One: a simple scheduled push from the customer's own rhythm, about 28 days by default, adjustable.
15Reorder in two tapsReorder, then Pay: same size, brand, address and payment.

The loop: step 15 lands back on step 8 with the usual order filled in, and the cycle repeats from Home. Predictive reminders from the real gas balance, and the smart regulator, are Phase Two.

Dealer journey: licence to settlement

The dealers who already deliver by phone take the order and deliver themselves. They join free: no commission (Navin, 1 Oct 2026). Ten steps from sign up to payout.

1Sign upPhone, OTP and a role: shop operator or driver. One app for both.
2OnboardKPDN licence upload with its expiry, SSM, bank account, radius, stock.
3Admin portalAdmin approvalAdmin checks the licence and SSM, then approves.
4Go onlineLocation is captured only while online, for nearest assignment.
5Incoming order45 second countdown. Decline or timeout sends it to the next nearest.
6Accept and dispatchJob detail with high-rise notes; pick the driver and truck.
7Status updatesOut for delivery, arrived, delivered. The customer sees each one.
8Photo proofMandatory at the door before the order can close.
9Cash for CODOn cash orders the driver marks the cash as collected.
10Wallet and settlementGas price and delivery fee kept in full; settlement history.

After step 10 the dealer stays online and the next order starts again at step 5. Fleet and stock (drivers and trucks as separate records, stock by brand and size) sit behind every step: .

Admin loop: the daily run

The VIN team's day in the admin portal (web, English, a super admin plus scoped sub-admins). The loop repeats every day.

1Approve suppliersNew dealers from the website form, licence and SSM checked.
2Watch live ordersEvery order with its full trail: offers, declines, status.
3Handle escalationsOrders declined four times in a row; call a dealer and assign by hand.
4Licence expiry alertsFlags before a KPDN licence lapses; an expired dealer gets no offers.
5Check coverageStatic supplier heat map with radius circles; gaps show at a glance.
6Settle dealersOnline payments paid out; fees on cash orders netted.
7Promos and rewardsPromo codes and points, one per order; fee and engine settings.
8ReportsThe day on the dashboard; Excel, CSV and print exports.

Step 8 closes the day and step 1 opens the next. Multi-step settlement approvals and full audit logs are Phase Two.
