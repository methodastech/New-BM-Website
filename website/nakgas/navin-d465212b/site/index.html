Siapa yang hantar gas saya?Who delivers my gas?

Pengedar berlesen KPDN yang paling dekat dengan anda dan ada stok. Anda nampak nama pengedar dan nombor plat lori, bukan nombor telefon peribadi.

The KPDN licensed dealer nearest you with stock. You see the dealer's name and the truck plate, never a personal phone number.

Berapa harganya?What does it cost?

Harga gas ialah harga kawalan KPDN, RM26.60 untuk tong 14 kg. Caj penghantaran ditetapkan oleh pengedar. Satu fi platform dikenakan bagi setiap pesanan. Semuanya ditunjukkan sebelum anda bayar.

The gas is the KPDN controlled price, RM26.60 for a 14 kg cylinder. The delivery fee is set by the dealer. One platform fee is charged per order. All of it is shown before you pay.

Boleh bayar tunai?Can I pay cash?

Boleh. Tunai semasa penghantaran, atau FPX, kad dan e-dompet dalam aplikasi.

Yes. Cash on delivery, or FPX, card and e-wallet in the app.

Saya tinggal di pangsapuri. Ada caj tambahan?I live in a high-rise. Is there an extra charge?

Alamat rumah tinggi menyimpan tingkat, lif dan unit supaya pemandu tahu sebelum bertolak. Sebarang caj tambahan ditetapkan oleh pengedar dan ditunjukkan sebelum anda bayar. Butirannya masih disahkan.

A high-rise address stores the floor, lift and unit so the driver knows before setting off. Any extra charge is set by the dealer and shown before you pay. The details are still being confirmed.

Boleh batalkan pesanan?Can I cancel an order?

Boleh, sebelum pengedar bertolak. Peraturan pembatalan ditunjukkan dalam aplikasi.

Yes, before the dealer sets off. The cancellation rules are shown in the app.

Di mana ia tersedia?Where is it available?

Johor Bahru dahulu. Kawasan lain menyusul apabila pengedar berlesen mendaftar.

Johor Bahru first. Other areas follow as licensed dealers sign up.

Bila aplikasi dilancarkan?When does the app launch?

Aplikasi sedang dibina. Pautan App Store dan Google Play aktif pada hari pelancaran, bermula di Johor Bahru.

The app is being built. The App Store and Google Play links go live on launch day, starting in Johor Bahru.
