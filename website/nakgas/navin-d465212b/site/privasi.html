What NakGas collects, why, who sees it, and your rights under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

Apa yang kami kumpulWhat we collect

  • Pelanggan: nombor telefon (untuk log masuk dengan kod SMS), nama, alamat penghantaran termasuk tingkat, lif dan unit untuk rumah tinggi, sejarah pesanan, penilaian dan komen.
  • Pengedar: nama perniagaan, nombor SSM, lesen KPDN dan tarikh tamatnya, butiran akaun bank untuk penyelesaian, pemandu dan lori, stok, dan lokasi semasa dalam talian sahaja.
  • Penghantaran: gambar bukti di pintu, masa setiap status, dan tunai yang dikutip.
  • Pembayaran: kad, FPX dan e-dompet diproses oleh satu gerbang pembayaran berlesen. Kami menyimpan rujukan transaksi, bukan nombor kad penuh.
  • Customers: phone number (to sign in with an SMS code), name, delivery address including floor, lift and unit for high-rise homes, order history, ratings and comments.
  • Dealers: business name, SSM number, KPDN licence and its expiry, bank account details for settlement, drivers and trucks, stock, and location while online only.
  • Deliveries: the proof photo at the door, the time of each status, and cash collected.
  • Payments: card, FPX and e-wallet are processed by one licensed payment gateway. We keep the transaction reference, not the full card number.

Kenapa kami gunaWhy we use it

  • Untuk menghantar pesanan anda kepada pengedar terdekat, menunjukkan status, menyimpan bukti penghantaran dan resit.
  • Untuk menghantar peringatan pesan semula, yang boleh anda ubah atau matikan.
  • Untuk menyemak lesen pengedar, menyelesaikan pembayaran dan menyelesaikan aduan.
  • To send your order to the nearest dealer, show its status, and keep the proof of delivery and the receipt.
  • To send reorder reminders, which you can change or switch off.
  • To check dealers' licences, settle payments and resolve complaints.

Siapa yang nampakWho sees it

  • Pengedar yang menerima pesanan anda nampak nama, alamat dan nota anda untuk penghantaran itu. Anda nampak nama pengedar dan nombor plat, bukan nombor telefon peribadi.
  • Kami tidak menjual data peribadi anda.
  • The dealer who accepts your order sees your name, address and note for that delivery. You see the dealer's name and plate, never a personal phone number.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Berapa lama disimpanHow long we keep it

  • Selagi akaun anda aktif, dan selepas itu hanya selama yang dikehendaki undang-undang untuk rekod perakaunan dan cukai. Tempoh tepat disahkan semasa semakan undang-undang.
  • For as long as your account is active, and after that only as long as the law requires for accounting and tax records. The exact periods are confirmed in the legal review.

Hak anda di bawah PDPA 2010Your rights under PDPA 2010

  • Meminta akses kepada data anda dan salinannya.
  • Membetulkan data yang salah.
  • Menarik balik persetujuan dan memadam akaun anda, kecuali rekod yang wajib disimpan.
  • Mengehadkan pemasaran: peringatan dan promosi boleh dimatikan di Tetapan.
  • Ask for access to your data and a copy of it.
  • Correct data that is wrong.
  • Withdraw consent and delete your account, except records we must keep by law.
  • Limit marketing: reminders and promotions can be switched off in Settings.

PersetujuanConsent

  • Borang pengedar dan borang hubungi meminta persetujuan anda sebelum dihantar. Aplikasi meminta persetujuan semasa mula, dan untuk lokasi hanya kepada pengedar semasa dalam talian.
  • The dealer form and the contact form ask for your consent before sending. The apps ask for consent at the start, and for location only from dealers while online.

Hubungi kami tentang privasiContact us about privacy

  • Pegawai perlindungan data dan alamat e-mel rasmi disahkan kemudian. Sehingga itu, gunakan halaman hubungi.
  • The data protection officer and the official email are to be confirmed. Until then, use the contact page.
