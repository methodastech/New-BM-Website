About Bangi Resort Hotel

A picturesque 5-star conference resort hotel, conveniently located midway between Kuala Lumpur International Airport and Kuala Lumpur city centre. Built atop Bandar Baru Bangi’s highest peak, it is surrounded by a 27-hole championship golf course, lush tropical greens and offers a wonderful vista and serene environment.

Make Yourself At Home In Our Hotel

Operated for 26 years, this resort hotel in Selangor has gained a strong reputation as a MICE hotel. The dedicated management and team are committed to providing excellent service and ensuring a delightful and memorable experience for all customers at Bangi Resort Hotel

ACCOMODATION

A Serene Retreat, With Great Convenience And Affordable Comfort.

Nestled on the peak of Bandar Baru Bangi, all our rooms feature varying scenic views overlooking our 27 hole golf course, the farm and the city lights of Bangi. Taking steps towards Eco-Responsibility, all our rooms come with bespoke amenities and feature a Go Green option for guests to reduce Single Use Plastics. Bangi Resort Hotel is the ideal choice for a relaxing stay in Selangor.
