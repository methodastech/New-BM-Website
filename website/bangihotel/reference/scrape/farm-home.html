CROWNING THE KING!

Experience the Reign of the King of Fruits at the Bangi Farm Durian World Championship!

Watch the highlights & drool!

Step into the thrilling world of durian at the recent 2-day Bangi Farm Durian World Championship, held at the prestigious Bangi Golf Resort. This spectacular event, captured in our latest video, was a true celebration of Malaysia's beloved "King of Fruits," bringing together enthusiasts, farmers, and experts for an unforgettable experience.

Day 1: A Feast for the Senses, Fierce Competition, and Historic Recognition!

The championship kicked off with an electrifying atmosphere, as participants geared up for the highly anticipated Durian Master and King of Kings competitions. Watch as skilled individuals demonstrate their prowess in identifying, selecting, and even de-husking durians with incredible speed and precision. The air was thick with excitement as contestants weighed their prized durians, vying for the coveted "Grand Champion: King of Kings" title.

Beyond the competitive arena, attendees were treated to insightful seminars led by industry experts, delving into the nuances of durian cultivation, varieties, and sustainable farming practices. The event also featured a vibrant farmers' market, showcasing an array of fresh durians and related products, allowing visitors to savor the diverse flavors and aromas of this unique fruit. A monumental highlight of the day was the recognition of Bangi Farm Resort with an ASEAN Record for hosting the First Regional-Level Durian Competition since 2018! This significant achievement underscores the event's importance and its role in re-establishing a premier platform for durian excellence in Southeast Asia. The day culminated in a delightful durian feast, where guests indulged in an endless supply of premium durians, fostering a true appreciation for this culinary gem.

Day 2: Fun, Fellowship, and Further Celebrations

The second day brought a different kind of energy with the "Durian Run," a unique and lively event that combined fitness with the love of durian. Participants, filled with enthusiasm, embarked on a fun run, proving that the love for durian can unite people in unexpected ways.

The festivities continued with the elegant Durian Gala Dinner and Awards Ceremony. This grand evening was a testament to the success of the durian industry, honoring outstanding achievements and contributions, including the special recognition of Bangi Farm Resort's groundbreaking ASEAN Record. From the passionate farmers to the innovative experts, everyone played a part in elevating the "King of Fruits" to new heights. The video showcases the joyful camaraderie, the excitement of the awards presentations, and the continued enjoyment of delectable durian dishes prepared in various creative ways.

A Celebration of Durian Excellence and Regional Leadership

The Bangi Farm Durian World Championship was more than just a competition; it was a vibrant festival celebrating the rich heritage and promising future of durian. With the majestic durians taking center stage, the event highlighted the dedication of local farmers, the passion of durian lovers, and the endless possibilities of this extraordinary fruit. The honouring of Bangi Farm Resort with the ASEAN Record for hosting the first regional-level durian competition since 2018 truly cemented this event's place in history, showcasing its pivotal role in spearheading durian excellence across Southeast Asia. Join us in reliving the highlights of this incredible 2-day event, a true tribute to the "King of Fruits"!
