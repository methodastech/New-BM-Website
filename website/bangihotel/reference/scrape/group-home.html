Bandar Baru Bangi: A Thriving Township at the Heart of Growth

Once a quiet locale, Bandar Baru Bangi has rapidly transformed into one of Malaysia’s most dynamic and vibrant townships.

Today, it stands as a testament to thoughtful development and strategic vision—boasting modern infrastructure, curated residential neighbourhoods, and a cluster of thriving commercial hubs that support a diverse and growing population.

Strategically situated just south of Kuala Lumpur, Bandar Baru Bangi enjoys excellent connectivity to key urban centers, making it a preferred location for families, professionals, investors, and entrepreneurs alike. The township has evolved into a center for education, innovation, business, and lifestyle, attracting prestigious universities, corporate offices, retail destinations, and recreational facilities.

Bandar Baru Bangi’s growth reflects a diverse blend of urban convenience and community-driven living.

It is a place where opportunity flourishes, where businesses can thrive, and where people can build meaningful, connected lives.

The potential in Bandar Baru Bangi continues to inspire our commitment to sustainable development, people-centered progress, and long-term value creation.

Bangi Golf Resort

Nestled in the heart of Basavorndar Baru Bangi, Bangi Golf Resort (BGR) stands as the choice golf and lifestyle destination to the community.

Known for its beautifully maintained 27-hole championship golf course, BGR is surrounded by lush greenery and scenic landscapes, offering both seasoned players and casual enthusiasts a leisurely golfing experience.

More than just a golf course, BGR is a vibrant hub for recreation, dining, and events. Its welcoming environment and tranquil setting make it a favourite not only amongst golfers but also families, corporate groups, and social guests seeking a place to unwind, celebrate, or connect.

The resort features purposefully designed event spaces that cater to weddings, banquets, and business functions, while its well-regarded dining venues serve a diverse array of local and international cuisines—making it a go-to destination for both weekday retreats and weekend escapes.

Strategically located in a fast-growing township just minutes from Kuala Lumpur, BGR reflects the perfect balance of recreation, refinement, and accessibility—a cornerstone of the lifestyle that defines Bandar Baru Bangi.
