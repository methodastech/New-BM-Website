About Bangi Golf Resort

Nestled in the heart of Basavorndar Baru Bangi, Bangi Golf Resort (BGR) stands as the choice golf and lifestyle destination to the community.Known for its beautifully maintained 27-hole championship golf course, BGR is surrounded by lush greenery and scenic landscapes, offering both seasoned players and casual enthusiasts a leisurely golfing experience.

More than just a golf course, BGR is a vibrant hub for recreation, dining, and events. Its welcoming environment and tranquil setting make it a favourite not only amongst golfers but also families, corporate groups, and social guests seeking a place to unwind, celebrate, or connect.

The resort features purposefully designed event spaces that cater to weddings, banquets, and business functions, while its well-regarded dining venues serve a diverse array of local and international cuisines—making it a go-to destination for both weekday retreats and weekend escapes.

Strategically located in a fast-growing township just minutes from Kuala Lumpur, BGR reflects the perfect balance of recreation, refinement, and accessibility—a cornerstone of the lifestyle that defines Bandar Baru Bangi.

A Complete Lifestyle Experience

The adjacent Bangi Resort Hotel offers a haven of comfort and convenience. Indulge in exquisite dining, versatile event spaces, and a range of wellness facilities—including a stunning pool, dedicated gym, and rejuvenating spa. For added leisure, enjoy active fun at our modern pickleball court.

With excellent accessibility via major highways (PLUS, LDP, SKVE, SILK) and a prime location between KLIA, Putrajaya, and Kuala Lumpur City Centre, Bangi Golf Resort is your ultimate destination to Play, Practice, Dine & Unwind in Style.

Anchored by Bangi Resort Hotel, the resort delivers a comprehensive experience for golfers and leisure travelers alike.
