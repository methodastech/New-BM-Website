The Flow
Three guests, three journeys, one front door.

Design the paths, not the pages

The estate serves three guests with different intents. Each has a path, and each path today hits a leak the audit named. The framework designs each one to end in a booking or a captured enquiry, on the estate’s own surfaces.

Persona 01 · the leisure guest

Books a stay, direct, with fees shown before payment

Google→homepage→one SwiftBook engine→deposit & parking disclosed→direct booking

Today she is split across two SwiftBook IDs and meets two fees at check-in. In the framework the deposit and parking sit in a Know Before You Book panel at rate and confirm, so she pays informed, on one engine, and the review complaints never happen.

Persona 02 · the event planner

Fills 1 of 28 function rooms through a captured enquiry

searches MICE venue→Meet→28 venues, capacities→enquiry form→captured lead

Today the events desk is a phone number and a wait. In the framework Meet opens a two-track panel, leisure and wedding on one side, conference and MICE on the other, and the enquiry lands in one pipeline the team owns, not a call queue.

Persona 03 · the group explorer

Discovers the estate and cross-sells himself

arrives for golf→the hub→farm · LaVista · dining→Stay & Play bundle→a longer visit

Today the hub reaches 3 of 7 destinations and the farm has no booking engine, so Traveloka sells the hotel-plus-farm staycation the group never offers direct. In the framework all seven destinations are first-class and the cross-sell rail offers the bundle on the owned estate.

The five-seconds, thirty-seconds, two-minutes rule

Every path is measured against one credibility rule. In five seconds the guest must know what the estate is and that it is trustworthy: one clear front door, the reputation visible. In thirty seconds they must find their intent: Stay, Meet, Play, Farm or Offers, one label each. In two minutes they must be able to act: book a room, enquire on an event, check a tee time, all reachable in one click from anywhere on the page.

Pin. Three guests, three paths, each ending in a booking or a captured enquiry on the estate’s own surface, not an OTA’s. Working proof · all three paths run in tab 03 · #estate, contact.html, group.html

Homepage
The band-by-band blueprint, the estate as an almanac.

An estate almanac, read from dawn to dusk

The design direction is the estate almanac: a warm, printed-journal reading of the 600 acres on ivory paper, poetic photo-and-fact chapters routed through clear, confident wayfinding. The signature is a dawn-to-dusk chapter that reads its light off a colour-temperature clock, closing on the estate-lake at dusk, whose lone tree rhymes with the gold tree wordmark. It looks nothing like a tech dashboard, and it delivers hard facts in a column that never moves. Type is Fraunces for display and numerals, General Sans for body and wayfinding. Gold is ink and hairline only, never a fill larger than a button.

The Spine
One booking and capture spine feeding the whole estate.

One spine, five surfaces

The audit’s deepest leak is a fragmented spine: two SwiftBook IDs, no email list, golf-only measurement. The framework replaces it with a single spine, one booking identity, one enquiry pipeline, one newsletter list, one analytics view with conversion events, feeding every surface on the estate.

The spine · one identity feeding hotel, golf, farm, arena and the hub
Booking

One SwiftBook identity

One canonical property ID everywhere, retiring the parallel ID and the dead FastBooking widget. The four-route sheet resolves Room, Golf, LaVista and Function to the right path. Canonical ID to confirm with Sam at discovery.

Capture

One list

A single email capture on every page, feeding one newsletter list with a welcome flow. Golf rates, festival dates, seasonal offers, member pricing, about once a month.

Enquiry

One pipeline

Every event and MICE enquiry lands in one pipeline the team owns, not scattered inboxes or a call queue.

Measurement

One GA4

Group-wide GA4 and Pixel with conversion events on booking, enquiry and capture, so direct-versus-OTA behaviour is finally visible across the estate.

Pin. One spine ends the fragmentation the audit measured, the two IDs, the missing list, the golf-only tracking, in a single stroke. Working proof · the single-engine booking flow and the one-list capture run in tab 03 · offers.html#booking and #member

3D & Motion
Four moments, and a performance contract.

Motion with a budget

Motion is restraint, not spectacle. There are four moments, and one hard contract they all sit inside. Everything is transform and opacity only, body copy never moves, there is one pinned band on the homepage, and the full page is complete and readable under prefers-reduced-motion.

Moment 01

The loader

Gold particles settle into a songket diamond then the tree wordmark, under 1.5 seconds, click to skip, released on dismiss. Reduced-motion gets a quick fade.

Moment 02

The hero

A still dusk-pool photo as the LCP image with a slow ken-burns, transform only. No video, none exists, so the still carries the first impression at full quality.

Moment 03

The dawn-to-dusk almanac

The one pinned band. A master scroll trigger walks a colour-temperature clock from dawn through golden hour to dusk while five photo chapters cross-fade and the facts sit still. Skippable by any menu anchor.

Moment 04

The footer diorama

A small dusk estate diorama with pointer parallax, contained in its own band above the footer, never a full-section canvas behind text.

The Build
Platform, roles, and the posture that keeps it clean.

Keep what works, remove what leaks

The prototype in tab 03 is static HTML for speed and portability. The real engagement keeps WordPress, which the team already runs and edits, so nothing about the platform is a rebuild. The work is optimisation on top of it.

Platform

WordPress, kept

The prototype is static; production stays on the WordPress the team knows. No migration, no retraining on a new system, no rebrand.

Governance

CMS roles & training

The team has no self-edit access today, so stale copy stays live. The build adds roles, a short training pass and a content owner, so the estate stays current.

Hygiene

The relic removed

WooCommerce 5.4.1 and its site-wide load come off. Divi, Slider Revolution and WP core are patched, generator strings suppressed.

Security posture. Exposed old-version strings on Divi 4.9.6 and a mid-2021 WooCommerce are a known attack surface. Patching the stack and removing the dead plugin is a security fix, not only a speed one, and it happens in week seven alongside tracking and CMS.
