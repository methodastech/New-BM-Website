Is this really ceremonial grade?

Yes. First harvest leaves, shade grown for weeks, then stone milled slowly so the flavour never burns off. Ceremonial describes the leaf and the process, not a price tag.

Why is it cheaper than imported packs?

We cut the middlemen. No import markups, no famous name premium, no distributor stacking margins. You pay for tea, not branding.

How many cups does a pack make?

About 8 cups from a 50g pack at roughly 6g a serving. That is about RM7.20 a cup for Zhu and RM10.20 for Ling.

Is shipping free?

Yes, on orders over RM50 to West Malaysia. Below that, shipping is a flat rate.

How should I store it?

Airtight, away from light and heat. After opening, keep it in the fridge and enjoy it within 4 to 8 weeks.

What if I do not like it?

Tell us. We stand behind every batch we mill and we will make it right.
