Is this a seminar?

No. You get a free screening, a 1 to 1 session, then a match. The webinars, Deal or Grill and #AskFAR are free and open to anyone.

Do I need capital?

Often less than you think. We look for undervalued units that need little or no money down. The free screening tells you where you stand.

What does membership cost?

RM2,500, RM5,000 or RM15,000, each excluding SST. The screening and the 5 tools cost nothing.

What if it does not work?

You get your money back. Pick 1 of the 6 written goals, take part for 24 months, and if you do not reach it we refund you.

Why is the price lower?

Because 50 screened buyers and more walk in together, with loans the developer already knows will be approved. We call it the Data Aggregated Community Buying Strategy.
