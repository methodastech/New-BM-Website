02 · Why This Work, and Why Now

An empty domain is the cheapest moment to build it right

  • The domain is new and nothing is live. yachtstylearabia.com was registered on 1 October 2026. There is no old site to migrate, no rankings to protect and no reader habit to break. Every decision is still open.
  • The group now has a home in Dubai. Heart Media Group became part of ITP Media Group, Dubai, announced on 3 September 2026. A title for Gulf readers is the first thing that reader will judge the group by.
  • Arabic is a build decision, not a translation. Right to left changes the navigation, every card, every caption and every icon that points. Designed in from the first page it costs days. Added to a finished English site it costs a rebuild.
  • A new title needs a position before it needs pages. You have named the reader: current owners and intending buyers, affluent, forty and over. What Yacht Style Arabia says to that reader in the Gulf, and how it sounds in two languages, are strategy questions. Answered first, they decide the home page. Answered later, they change it.
  • A daily title is won in search, and now in AI answers. At one to two stories a day, structure decides whether each story is found and whether an AI engine credits the title when it answers a question about a yacht. Built in at the start, it is part of the build. Added to a live archive, it means reworking every story.
Why we recommend Choice B: Choice A is a complete title site, built on the existing Yacht Style look and a core strategy. Choice B gives the Arabia title what a new title in a new market needs: the full brand and business strategy, a web identity of its own in Latin and Arabic script, a CMS with accounts, roles and scheduling for a newsroom that publishes daily, and room for 15 pages and more. It is RM39,000 of work for RM25,000.

05 · The Likely Questions

The likely questions, answered directly

The two prices

Why two choices and not one figure?

Because the scope is yours to set. Both include the editorial CMS in English and Arabic. Choice A is the ten page title site with a core strategy. Choice B is the larger site with the full strategy, an identity for the Arabia title and a CMS for a newsroom. Section 4.1 shows the difference box by box.

Later

Can we start with Choice A and move up later?

Yes. Our list carries an upgrade credit: if you move to the higher tier later, a portion of what you have paid is credited toward the new build. Taken now, Choice B also brings the full strategy and the identity at no charge.

Timing

Is three or four weeks realistic for a bilingual site?

Yes, when the content, the strategy intake and one approver are ready at kickoff. Choice A is complete in two weeks and Choice B in three. Each then has one full week kept for testing, your amendments and fixes, so nothing goes live in a rush. Slow content is the most common cause of delay on any website, not the build. Section 4.2 lists exactly what we need and when.

Arabic

Who writes the Arabic?

You do. We build the Arabic edition, set its type and mirror every layout, and your editors or translators supply and approve the words. We do not publish machine translation under your masthead.

The modules

What about a yacht directory, an issue reader or charter enquiries?

They are later stages, listed in section 3.1, each quoted before it starts and added to the live site without a rebuild. At launch the home page carries bands for charter, the magazine, events and partners: on Choice B each links to its own simple page, on Choice A to the matching section and the Advertise page.

Search and AI

What does SEO and GEO friendly mean in this build?

SEO is being found on Google. GEO, generative engine optimisation, is being read and cited by AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. Both rest on the same foundations: clean structure, structured data on every story, named authors, dated facts, fast pages and sitemaps that update as you publish. All of it is in section 4.1 and inside both prices. What a build cannot do is the monthly work of research, links and optimisation: that is our Search & Visibility service, in section 3.1.

Daily publishing

Can the site carry one to two stories a day?

Yes. Editors fill fields and publish; the home page, the section pages, the sitemaps and the feeds update on their own. On Choice B stories are scheduled ahead, each editor has an account and a role, and the cover story is pinned by hand. At that pace the archive holds 365 to 730 stories after one year, which is why search, tags and the section pages are in the build from the first day.

The references

Will it look like the four websites we sent?

It will stand beside them, not copy them. All four are white pages with serif headlines, led by one large photograph, with named sections below and advertising built into the layout, and that is the direction we design to. None of the four is published in Arabic. What Yacht Style Arabia adds is its own: the Gulf as home water, and an Arabic edition designed right to left. You see the home page as a design preview in week 1 and approve it before a page is coded.

Maintenance

Who maintains the site once it is live?

Your team, or us on a Website Care maintenance plan, section 3.2. Every plan has a person on our team checking the site each working day, security patches, backups we restore for you and bug fixes. Growth adds speed work and small content updates; Strategic adds a check every 60 seconds and monthly design improvements. Plans run month to month with 30 days notice, or a year at the price of ten months.

Ownership

Who owns the site and the strategy?

You do. Code, domain, content, accounts, the strategy document and the identity files are handed over in full on final payment. Your editors publish from the first day, with no developer in between.
