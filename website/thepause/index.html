Scent 101

The essence, explained.

Every bottle is a small architecture. Touch a point to read it.

An essence is the concentrated soul of a plant. Petals, resin and bark, distilled to a few honest drops. It unfolds in three acts.

01

The top note

The first breath. Bright and quick to fade, it opens the room in seconds. Citrus, cool herbs, clean air.

Felt in the first ten minutes
02

The heart note

The character of the scent. It arrives after minutes and holds for hours. Florals, soft spice, green leaves.

Carries the room through the evening
03

The base note

What lingers. Low and warm, it is the note your room remembers long after the flame is out. Woods, resin, amber.

Stays in fabric and memory for days
04

The ritual

Six to eight drops for a medium room, or one lit candle. Let it work for twenty minutes, then let it rest. A scent needs silence too.
