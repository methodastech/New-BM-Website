Care and use

Kept well, kept long.

Gentle objects last when they are treated gently. A few quiet notes on care, shipping, and returns.

Caring for your pieces

Trim the wick to 5mm before each light. Burn for at least an hour the first time so the pool reaches the edge. Keep away from drafts, and never leave a flame unattended.
Place the reeds in the oil and let them draw. Flip the reeds every few days to refresh the scent. Keep away from direct sun and heat.
Light the tip, let it catch, then gently blow out the flame. Rest it on a heatproof holder, open a window slightly, and let the smoke do the rest.
Wipe clean with a soft, dry cloth. Each piece is natural stone, so veins and tone vary. That variation is the point.
Two or three presses into the air, or over soft furnishings. Let it settle for a moment before you breathe it in.

Shipping

Every order is packed with care and usually dispatched within three to five business days. Complimentary shipping applies to orders over RM 250 within Malaysia.

Candles and jade pieces travel wrapped and cushioned. If anything arrives less than perfect, tell us and we will make it right.

Returns

Unopened items can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Write to us first and we will walk you through it, gently.

Because fragrance is personal and hygiene matters, opened or burned pieces cannot be returned. If you are unsure before buying, the Scent and Space Quiz is a quiet way to choose well the first time.
