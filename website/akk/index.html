A.01 About AKK Alliance

Built on Experience. Driven by Safety.

AKK Alliance Sdn Bhd is a Malaysian lift and escalator solutions company established in 2001. The company provides comprehensive services covering installation, modernisation, maintenance, servicing, technical support and proactive care for vertical transportation systems.

Over two decades, AKK has built its reputation on safe, reliable and efficient delivery, serving property management, building maintenance and construction sectors. Our work is anchored by a dedicated technical team, preventive maintenance discipline and long-term client relationships.
