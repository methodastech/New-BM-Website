A portfolio showcase built around a natural, premium visual system for JB Cocoa, using strong product imagery and launch-ready campaign assets.

Building a richer cocoa story

Imagine the first moment a customer sees a cocoa brand and immediately understands the depth behind it: the farm, the craft, the supply chain, the quality standard, and the care that makes every product feel purposeful.

For JB Cocoa, the brand experience needed to feel grounded, natural, and globally credible. Our direction translated the warmth of cocoa cultivation into a refined visual system that feels premium without losing its agricultural foundation.

From origin to recognition

At JB Cocoa, every design element was shaped to reflect trust, consistency, and growth. From the logo application to the supporting brand assets, each touchpoint was built to make the brand easier to recognise across presentations, campaigns, packaging, and event materials.

The visual language balances rich cocoa imagery with clean brand structure, helping the company communicate both industry expertise and product quality with clarity.

Designed for practical brand use

The goal was not only to create beautiful visuals. The system had to work across real brand conditions: corporate collateral, launch communications, event assets, and everyday touchpoints that support the sales and marketing team.

By keeping the visual foundation flexible, JB Cocoa can present itself consistently while still adapting to different formats, audiences, and campaign needs.
