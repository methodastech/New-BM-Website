Standard package terms.

Draft 2026-11-15-draft-4. These terms are a proposal for review. They are not in force, they have not had legal review, and no package is on sale.

The packages

  • Launch: 1 page. US$19 / RM79 for your first month, then US$19 / RM79 a month.
  • Grow: Up to 5 pages. US$99 / RM399 for your first month, then US$49 / RM199 a month.
  • Own It: Up to 8 pages. US$465 / RM1,900 once. No monthly fee.

Free with every package

  • Competitor analysis: Name up to 3 competitors. We show where your website can beat theirs.
  • Website conversion plan: 1 page on what your site must say and do to turn visitors into enquiries.
  • SEO and AIO optimisation: Titles, descriptions, speed, structure and structured data, set up so Google and AI search can read your site.
  • Logo Health Check: We check how your logo holds up on a website and tell you plainly what we see.

To be confirmed before launch: when each free item is delivered, and in what form.

Your brief and your references

We build your website from the brief you give us. References, whether our builds or links you paste, guide the look of your site. They do not buy a copy of another website, and they do not buy a fully custom design: that is the Premium service.

Price and billing

Launch: US$19 / RM79 is charged on the day you order and covers your first month. US$19 / RM79 is then charged each month.

Grow: US$99 / RM399 is charged on the day you order. It covers your first month and the setup of up to 5 pages. US$49 / RM199 is then charged each month.

Own It: US$465 / RM1,900 is charged once. There is no subscription.

Launch and Grow can be paid in US dollars or Malaysian ringgit. You are charged in the currency shown on your order, and renewals stay in that currency. Own It is quoted in the currency you choose and confirmed on your invoice. Prices exclude the optional domain add-on, other optional extras and applicable taxes. The amount due today and the renewal amount are shown before you pay.

Founding rate

Where a package shows an original price and a founding rate, the founding rate is the price you pay. 15 years in, grant-backed. We pass our tech grants straight to clients, so our websites are at founding-rate pricing this quarter. Limited slots.

Renewal

A subscription renews each month on the same day until you cancel it. Your billing dashboard shows the next charge date and amount.

Money-back guarantee

We agree the delivery date and the scope with you before work starts. If we miss the agreed delivery date, or the final delivery is materially incomplete against the agreed scope, you get a full refund. A delay that comes from your side moves the delivery date with it.

Lifetime tech support

Questions are answered and small tweaks are handled for as long as you use the site, at no charge. New pages, new features and redesigns are quoted separately.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from your billing dashboard. Your site stays live until the end of the period you have paid for. After that date the managed site is taken offline.

To be confirmed before launch: how long your content stays available for export after the site goes offline, and what the export contains.

Ownership

You own your domain and the text, logo and images you supply. On Launch and Grow the website runs on Brand Method's platform while your subscription is active. If you need the website files and a full handover, choose Own It.

Domains

Each managed site includes a Brand Method subdomain. You may connect a domain you already own, at no extra cost, once you verify that it is yours. Its registration and renewal charges from your registrar stay yours.

If you ask us to in your brief, we register a domain for you for a one time fee of US$25 / RM100. A .com or .com.my, registered in your own name and connected to your website. We check the name is free and confirm it with you before we register it. On Launch and Grow, renewal of that domain is included while your subscription is active. On Own It, the first year is included and later renewals are yours. The domain is registered to you and stays yours if you cancel. Renewals after you cancel are yours.

Edits and support

You can change permitted text, images and contact details from your dashboard. Every Standard package includes up to 3 amendments. Design changes, new functions and new pages beyond your package are separate services, quoted before any work starts.

To be confirmed before launch: support hours, the reply target, and the exact limits on edits.

Not included

Custom design, a CMS, online stores, bookings, portals, ad management, monthly content, logo design, email inboxes and legal policies are not part of the Standard packages.

Own It

Own It is the Startup Website package published on the Brand Method pricing page, at US$465 / RM1,900. Its scope, guarantees and payment terms are the ones shown there. Nothing on this page changes them.
