Questions, answered plainly.

Start my briefhello@brandmethod.co
Do I have to choose a design?

No. You tell us about your business and show us sites you like. Our studio builds yours from that brief.

Is it really US$19 / RM79?

Yes. Launch is US$19 / RM79 for the first month and US$19 / RM79 at each monthly renewal. You see the full amount before you pay.

What does the price cover?

Your website, built by our studio from your brief, with 4 free extras, a money-back guarantee and lifetime tech support. A domain we register for you is in your own name, so it always stays yours. If tax applies where you are, you see it before you pay. Read the package terms

How does the money-back guarantee work?

We agree the delivery date and the scope with you first. If we miss either, you get a full refund. If a delay comes from your side, the date simply moves with it.

What is lifetime tech support?

Questions answered and small tweaks handled for as long as you use the site, at no charge.

Can I pay in ringgit or US dollars?

Yes. Switch the prices to the currency you prefer. You are charged in the currency shown on your order.

What comes free?

Every package comes with competitor analysis, website conversion plan, SEO and AIO optimisation and a Logo Health Check. They are part of the package, not an extra to buy later.

Can I use one of your builds as a reference?

Yes. Pick up to 3 from our portfolio, or paste links to sites you like. References guide the look. A fully custom design is our Premium service.

Do I need my own domain?

No. If you have a domain, we connect it at no extra cost. If you do not, we register one in your name for US$25 / RM100 once, and renewal is included while your plan is active. You can also start on a free Brand Method address and decide later.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel from your billing dashboard. Your website stays live through the period you have paid for.

Who owns the website?

You own your domain and your content. On Launch and Grow the site runs on our platform while your plan is active. Choose Own It if you want the code and the files.

Will it appear on Google and in AI answers?

We set up SEO and AIO, so search engines and AI answers such as ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews can read your site. They decide if and when to show it. Results are not guaranteed.

That is Premium: custom design, a CMS and high quality SEO. See the 3 Premium packages
