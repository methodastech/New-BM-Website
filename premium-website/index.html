Before you ask.

Build a website with ushello@brandmethod.co
How long does it take?

A Business Website in 10 days, a Growth Website in 2 weeks, a Corporate Website & Portal in about 3 weeks. Your launch date is fixed in writing before work starts.

What is the money-back guarantee?

If we miss the agreed date or the agreed scope, you get a full refund. It covers the Business Website and the Growth Website. The Corporate Website & Portal is paid by milestone.

What comes free?

Every Premium package comes with high quality SEO and AIO optimisation free, worth US$2,000 / RM5,000, a CMS, a competitor analysis and a website conversion plan.

Will my site show up on Google and in AI answers?

Every site is built with SEO and AIO optimisation: clean structure, fast pages, structured data and clear answers that Google, ChatGPT and AI Overviews can read. They decide what to show, so we do not promise a position.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes. Every package includes a CMS, so you change text, images and posts without a developer.

What support do I get after launch?

Lifetime tech support, free. Questions answered and small tweaks handled for as long as you use the site.

Can you build more than a website?

Yes. Scoped projects cover online stores, apps, portals, CRM, AI automation, AI assistants and data crawlers.

Do I own the website?

Our custom website packages specify the handover of code, domain and content. Your proposal confirms the agreed scope and any third party licences.

Is hosting or a care plan mandatory?

No. An owned custom build does not require an ongoing Brand Method care contract unless you choose one. Outside hosting and software costs may still apply.

Can I apply for a government grant?

Eligibility depends on a currently open official scheme and on your business and project. We can discuss your digitalisation scope, but we do not promise funding, official endorsement or eligibility.

I only need a simple website.

Our Standard packages start at US$19 / RM79 a month, or US$465 / RM1,900 once to own it. See the 3 Standard packages

A short brief is enough. We will come back with a recommended scope.
