# FREEDOM — THE GAME CONCEPT
*One page. Everything in the game answers to this.*

---

## The one-liner
**You are a freelance pilot defending THE HAVEN — a giant trader ship that is your home, hangar, and only friend — through 20 waves of hostile space, in an era where weapons don't throw bullets, they bend physics.**

## The fantasy
It's 2085. Kinetics are dead — shields shrug off mass, and the Ejecta Accords made stray bullets a war crime. Guns are now **CE arms** (coherent energy) or **RIFTWORK** — clan-smithed weapons that fold spacetime: pocket black holes, tethers, time bubbles. You fly a small ship you could actually live in (there's a bunk behind the reactor), working a defense contract for a trader too big and too rich to die quietly.

## One wave of FREEDOM (the loop)
1. **LAUNCH** from the Haven's hangar.
2. **DOGFIGHT** — a few *dangerous* ships, not a swarm. They dash, manage shields, lead their shots — everything you can do, they can do. Strangers are **grey until they identify** — then their faction colors bleed in.
3. **CHOOSE YOUR KILLS** — this is the heart:
   - **Vaporize** it → credits only, nothing survives.
   - **Kill it clean** → loot what its systems dropped.
   - **Cripple it** → tractor the hulk home for its *full* cargo and modules.
   - **Board it** → walk in with your sidearm and take everything, if you survive.
4. **RETURN** — a story card waits on the flight home (beggars, swindlers, clan gifts — choices that bend the next wave).
5. **DOCK** — sell cargo, fit modules into your rooms, train a skill, hire crew, **rest** (once, for a price), and watch the Haven **spend its own income upgrading itself**.
6. Launch again. Boss every 5 waves. Wave 20 is **your Shadow — a mirror of your own peak build**.

## The pillars (what makes it addictive)
- **Every kill is a decision** (money now vs salvage vs risk).
- **Every wave ends in a story choice** with teeth.
- **Every dock visit visibly grows two things**: your ship and the Haven.
- **Consequence is real**: rooms break and must be welded mid-fight, factions remember, cargo spills when you die, your Shadow remembers your best day.

## The systems (all built)
| System | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Flight** | Mouse aims, **W** burns, **S** brakes to a dead stop. Boost (Shift) and dash (Space) drink energy. Nova-Drift momentum. |
| **Weapons** | LMB = CE arm · RMB = second CE arm (when fitted) · **Q = rift arm** (8 unlockable: Singularity Driver, Grav Sling, Arc Lattice, Repulsor Shear, Phase Lance, Lag Field, Rift Mine, Event Tether). |
| **The ship inside** | Tab = inventory + FTL-style deck plan. 9 rooms on the starter (Helm, Lance Bay, Rift Bay, Energy Core, Shield, Life Support, Quarters, Tractor, Engine); bigger hulls = more rooms. Rooms hold visible **modules** (charge/homing/nova/spread + 28 more). Rooms take damage; hold **R** to weld. |
| **Two inventories** | **Ship hold**: cargo (sellables) separate from ship parts (modules/weapons). **On your person**: boarding gear, personal weapons, armor — what you carry when you walk. |
| **Pilot** | 8 skills to rank 10 with breakthroughs (Engineering weld, Combat unlocks by deed, Charisma, Entrepreneur…). Experience per run, credits persist. |
| **People** | Crew (gunner/engineer/quartermaster/shieldtech/bruiser), faction REP with memory (Imperial · Civilian · Clan) — be deputized or go **outlaw** (Black Market, smuggling, hunted waves). |
| **The Haven** | 400 hull + shields, visibly degrades and recovers. Earns trader cuts/stipends/haul taxes + your donations and **upgrades itself at dock**. It is the hangar — "the hangar can be anywhere, one day." |
| **Campaign** | 20 waves · rotation of defend / sortie / haul · bosses at 5/10/15/20 (Undercutter, Warlord, **Broodmother**, **Your Shadow** + true ending) · REP-branched waves 11/18/19. |
| **Codex** | One CODEX hub documents everything — weapons, ships, rooms, modules, factions, salvage law, the era — rendered from live game data so it can never lie. |

## Controls (final)
**Mouse** aim · **LMB** fire / **RMB** second gun · **Q** rift arm · **W** thrust / **S** brake · **Shift** boost · **Space** dash · **R** weld · **G** tractor · **E** board · **Tab** inventory · **Esc** menu · **P** pause (unlimited) · **C** control mode · **B** warp home

## What "done" means (the finish line)
A fresh player can: start, learn from the how-to in 30 seconds, survive wave 1 on skill, feel every upgrade, fear every boss, beat wave 20, meet their Shadow, and immediately press NEW GAME — with zero errors, no UI overlap, and every system explained in the codex.
