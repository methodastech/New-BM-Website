Fly with WASD, aim with your mouse, and hold the line while traders pay fees, disabled ships call maydays, and bounties pile up. Spend it all between waves on your ship and the station itself. Every fifth wave, a boss shows up , and the last one looks a lot like you.

Field manual
How to play
Fly & aimYour mouse aims the ship — the nose tracks the pointer. HOLD W to burn toward it; let go and you coast on momentum. HOLD S to retro-brake to a dead stop. Shift boosts (drains energy), Space is a knife-dash toward the cursor (costs energy). Press C to cycle control feel (cursor / strafe / thrust).
ShootTap or hold to fire. Charge weapons (Blaster, Ion Cannon, Nova Lock) build a heavy shot while held. Right-click fires your SECOND CE hardpoint once one is fitted at the dock. Q deploys the Rift Bay: the Singularity Driver collapses a micro black hole that drags hulls, bends every bolt in flight — yours too — and crushes what it catches.
Defend THE HAVENPirates come for the mothership, not just you. Watch her integrity bar up top , if her hull hits zero, the run ends.
Field repairsTrain the Engineering skill, then HOLD R to weld your hull mid-fight. Your engines idle and the glow draws attention , pick your moment.
EarnBounties, docking fees from traders, and rescued maydays all pay. Tow disabled ships into the Haven's dock ring for the biggest rewards.
UpgradeBetween waves you dock at the Haven. Spend credits on your ship, weapons and the station; skills train free from combat XP.
BossesEvery fifth wave a boss arrives — four in all. The first three unlock weapon tiers; the last one flies your own hull.
PauseP freezes the action; Esc opens the pause menu (resume / how-to / quit). Pause as often and as long as you like — it is unlimited.
