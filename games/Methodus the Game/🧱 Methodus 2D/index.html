Field manual
How to play
AimMove your mouse. The ship always faces your cursor.
ShootTap to fire a single shot. Hold to charge a heavy bolt, then release.
ShieldsClick your own service nodes to gain shield and a little hull. Each node then cools down.
SurviveOutlast each wave, then take down the boss that comes for your brand.
PausePress P or Esc, or tap the pause button, any time during a run.
Have funIt is an arcade , experiment, take risks, and chase a high score. The best parts are the ones you discover for yourself.
