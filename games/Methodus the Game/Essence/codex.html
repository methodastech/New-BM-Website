§0 Overview

Essence is a fast, dangerous glass-cannon combat game. You cast magic by speaking or typing incantations — never by a hotkey — chanting an element up its power ladder, then naming the spell to loose it. Between fights you gather wood and stone, and raise your own fort Valheim-style. Skeletons rise in waves at the red grave.

100
Physical life. 0 = death.
✨ Essence
100
One bar: stamina + raw mana.
🗣️ Cast trigger
Word
Voice or typed only.
🪵🪨 Materials
2
Wood & stone.

Two documents feed this codex. The design spec describes a deep magic economy — Contained Magic separate from Essence, Magic Hearts, staff capacity, an E→S tier ladder, containment timers and fusion recipes. The current build implements a leaner, faster version of that vision (a single essence bar, a three-rung D→C→B ladder, five elements, plus a whole building & world layer the spec never described). Where they differ, this codex says so plainly.

§5 Winded & Arcane Collapse Locked design

The final source that drives Essence to zero decides the state.

😮‍💨 If PHYSICAL → Winded
Conscious · slow walk only · no sprint/dodge/attack/block/conversion · no HP loss · recover at 25 essence.
😵 If MAGIC → Arcane Collapse
Faints · all defence disabled · contained magic disperses · −20% max HP · wake at 25 essence · lethal if HP ≤ 20.
🔮 If SPELL CAST
No zero-essence state — casts spend contained magic, not essence.
Build status: not yet implemented. Running out of essence today simply gates further spending ("too winded to sprint") without the Winded / Collapse states or the HP penalty. This is a priority target from the spec.

§6 Magic Hearts Locked design

A normal character has one Magic Heart = one active reserve. You may hold Fire or Water, not both — unless a fusion recipe transforms the single reserve. A second Heart permits two reserves whose amounts share total capacity.

Sum(all active reserve amounts) <= total magic capacity
Divergence: the build has no Magic Heart or reserve object at all — one essence bar, one rite kindled at a time.

§9 Incantation & chants In game

Magic has exactly two triggers: speak it (voice, V) or type it (T). No hotkey, no click, ever casts. Hold click only aims the throw; the word looses it.

§10 Containment & dispersal Locked design

containment_seconds = 300 × 2^(staff_level)   # levels 0–2
Body 300s · Staff-1 600s · Staff-2 1200s · Staff-3+ infinite while equipped

Reaching a new tier or completing a fusion resets the timer to the current equipment limit. Casting a spell does not reset it. When the timer hits zero, all remaining magic disperses. Dropping a no-dispersal staff caps remaining time at 300s.

Build: no held reserve means no containment timer — a rite is chanted and cast in one exchange.

§12 Spell unlock & scaling

Minimum-tier rule Locked design

A spell never casts below its unlock tier. Fireball unlocks at E (casts E→S). Fire Arrow unlocks at D (no E version). Freeze unlocks at C. In the build the rule is now universal: every form carries an unlock rung — Cyclone, Bone Cage and Flamethrower need C, Raise Dead stays B-only, and a refused cast names the tier it demands.

§21–23 Fusion — Ice · Explosion · Thunder

Fusion transforms one reserve through a sequence of element tokens in a single uninterrupted climb. A recognised full sequence yields a fusion element; a recognised prefix yields an intermediate Tainted state.

§13 Damage model Locked design

Glass-cannon target: against 100 HP, short-sword light ≈ 5 hits, long-sword light ≈ 3–4, Fireball E ≈ 3–4, Fireball D ≈ 2. High tiers can one-shot.

RawDamage  = round(E_base × QualityMult[tier])
FinalDamage= max(1, round(Raw × HitLocation × (1 − Resistance)))
HitLocation: arm/leg 0.75 · torso 1.00 · head/organ 1.50
Build: uses FormBase × TierMult × WeaponMult with no hit-location or armour term yet (the resistance hook is a planned add). Crits are a flat ~12% ×1.6 roll; staggered foes take ~×1.8.

§— Foes & the risen In game

Not in the v1.0 spec — the build's enemy layer.

💀 Skeleton raiders
Rise in waves. Slash & heavy combos, block, and a gold-eye windup telegraph — the parry window.
💨 They dodge
Read your longsword windup and hop to the flank (~26%, +2%/wave). Stunned / staggered / mid-swing foes cannot.
🪓 They siege
Can't reach you? They batter the wood — walls, doors, whatever blocks the path.
🧟 Raise Dead ally
Your own risen guard holds its ground 120 s, respects shut doors, returns to rest on your death.

Placement physics

🧲 Snapping
2 m grid, 10 m reach, audible cell-tick + ghost pop. R cycles pieces, wheel rotates.
📐 Stacking
Pieces snap on top of each other; walls make storeys, floors/roofs cap them, doors bear a wall above.
🏗️ Support
What loses its bearing collapses, storey by storey. Nothing hangs in empty air.
♻️ Break = refund
Anything destroyed, collapsed, smashed or demolished bursts and returns its materials.

§30 Locked decisions (spec) Locked design

100 HP · 100 Essence · essence is both stamina and mana.
Essence regen 2/s; continues while holding magic and speaking.
Low warning at 25; recovery from zero at 25.
Physical zero = Winded; magical zero = Arcane Collapse (−20% max HP, magic disperses).
Body capacity 25; staffs the only capacity gear, two-handed.
Tier quality never downgrades; equipment loss cuts quantity, never earned quality.
Stage costs +25/+30/+35/+40/+45 through A; spell cost +1 per tier above unlock.
Ice begins at C (Freeze); Explosion at B after Water-Water-Fire-Fire.
