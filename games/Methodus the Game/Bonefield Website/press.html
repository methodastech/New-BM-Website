The Pitch

Describe it in one breath

Short (one line)

A Vampire-Survivors-meets-Sekiro action-roguelite where you cast spells by speaking the incantation aloud.

Medium

Born of grief, you descend an endless dungeon, devouring power and hiding among the people who hunt you. Swing steel like a knight, or chant long incantations to unleash an apocalypse — and invent your own spells by combining spirits in the Forge. Mispronounce the words and the spell fizzles, weakens, or detonates in your own face.

The Hook

Key features

  • Speak to cast. Say the incantation aloud; each correct phrase charges a higher tier, then a release word unleashes it.
  • Invent your spells. Combine spirits in the Forge into spells that did not exist a moment ago, then name and share them.
  • Word & blade. A warrior snaps off fast spells; a mage chants for world-ending power. Where you sit is your identity.
  • Learn by consuming. You don't shop for upgrades, you eat them. Progression is predation.
  • The double life. Monster below, “human” above, wearing a stolen face among those who hunt you.
  • Mastery you can hear. Cleaner pronunciation hits harder — the skill is real, and it's yours.
