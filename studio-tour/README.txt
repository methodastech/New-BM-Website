Brand Method office tour: the 3D section alone

Upload this folder as it is (for example as studio-tour/ on brandmethod.co).
The tour itself is projects/bm-tour/index.html. Put it on any page in an iframe:

  <iframe src="studio-tour/projects/bm-tour/index.html"
          title="A 3D tour from MRANTI Park to the Brand Method office"
          loading="lazy" style="width:100%;height:680px;border:0;display:block"></iframe>

Its background is see-through, so the page's own background (the contact page's grid) shows round the model.
