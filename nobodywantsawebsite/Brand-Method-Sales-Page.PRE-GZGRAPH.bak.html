Work starts within 24 hours of kickoff, not next month. A Starter site ships in about a week, websites in 1 to 3 weeks, a scoped app in about 3 weeks. The sooner it is live, the sooner it stops losing you enquiries. Bigger builds get a milestone timeline agreed at kickoff.
Is the work just AI-generated?
No. The strategy, design and craft are done by senior people. We use our own system to remove the busywork so we move faster and price fairer, but what you get is real, human, considered work.
What's actually included?

Almost certainly. We've built 100+ brands across F&B, retail, healthcare, finance, tourism, property and more. The method works because it starts with your customers, not an industry template.

A kickoff, a few approvals at the milestones, and a launch. We do the heavy lifting and keep you updated at every step, so you stay in control of the direction without it eating your week.
Do I own everything, or am I renting my own business?
You own all of it: code, domain, content, brand files and customer data, yours from day one. No vendor lock, no platform that holds your leads where you cannot reach them. It is an asset on your books, not a subscription you are afraid to cancel.
