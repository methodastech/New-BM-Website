## Make it human (the notes that matter)

- She talks to **one person**, like a client across the table. Calm, not hyped. A small real smile, not a presenter face.
- Let her stumble-proof lines breathe. Short sentences, one breath each. Cut the takes where she sounds like she is reading.
- The hook is the whole game. Shoot 4 to 6 versions of the first two lines and test them.
- Captions are her actual words, always on. People watch muted.

## Production

- 9:16, 1080×1920. Keep her face and text out of the top 12% and bottom 18%.
- Brand blue `#2536F5`, white, ink. Fraunces for big words, JetBrains Mono for numbers.
- Lower-third name tag the first time she is on cam: "Nuramina Afham · CCRO".
- Wardrobe: the blue blazer + white shirt. Plain light-grey or blue backdrop so the dot graphics read.

## Shot list (one short session)

- A — Hook (2 lines), tight, 4 to 6 takes.
- B — Insight, same framing.
- C — The shift, slightly wider, hand can mark the three points.
- D — Action, warm, leaning in.
